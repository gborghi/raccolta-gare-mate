*Jetons sur un polygone à 1997 côtés, somme 1 : peut-on tout ramasser ?*

> **Exercice I**
>
> On a placé un jeton sur chaque sommet d'un polygone régulier à $1997$ côtés. Sur chacun de ces jetons est inscrit un entier relatif, la somme de ces entiers relatifs étant égale à $1$. On choisit un sommet de départ et on parcourt le polygone dans le sens trigonométrique en ramassant les jetons au fur et à mesure, tant que la somme des entiers inscrits sur les jetons ramassés est strictement positive.
>
> Peut-on choisir le sommet de départ de façon à ramasser tous les jetons ? Si oui, combien y a-t-il de choix possibles ?

*Gettoni su un poligono di 1997 lati, somma 1: si possono raccogliere tutti?*

> **Esercizio I**
>
> Si è posto un gettone su ogni vertice di un poligono regolare di $1997$ lati. Su ciascuno di questi gettoni è scritto un intero relativo, e la somma di questi interi relativi è uguale a $1$. Si sceglie un vertice di partenza e si percorre il poligono in senso antiorario, raccogliendo i gettoni via via, finché la somma degli interi scritti sui gettoni raccolti è strettamente positiva.
>
> Si può scegliere il vertice di partenza in modo da raccogliere tutti i gettoni? Se sì, quanti sono i modi possibili?

*Tokens on a 1997-gon, sum 1: can one collect them all?*

> **Exercise I**
>
> A token has been placed on each vertex of a regular polygon with $1997$ sides. On each of these tokens an integer is written, the sum of these integers being equal to $1$. One chooses a starting vertex and traverses the polygon anticlockwise, collecting the tokens one after another, as long as the sum of the integers written on the collected tokens is strictly positive.
>
> Can one choose the starting vertex so as to collect all the tokens? If so, how many possible choices are there?

*λ(α) reliant sommes de puissances des côtés et des médianes*

> **Exercice IV**
>
> Étant donné un triangle $ABC$, on note $a$, $b$, $c$ les longueurs de ses côtés et $m$, $n$, $p$ les longueurs de ses médianes.
>
> Pour tout réel $\alpha$ strictement positif, on définit le réel $\lambda(\alpha)$ par la relation
>
> $$a^{\alpha} + b^{\alpha} + c^{\alpha} = \lambda(\alpha)\,(m^{\alpha} + n^{\alpha} + p^{\alpha}).$$
>
> 1. Calculer $\lambda(2)$.
> 2. Calculer la limite de $\lambda(\alpha)$ lorsque $\alpha$ tend vers $0$.
> 3. À quelle condition portant sur $a$, $b$, $c$ le réel $\lambda(\alpha)$ est-il indépendant de $\alpha$ ?

*For a fixed oriented angle alpha, find the locus Gamma of point C seeing AB under that angle, show the centroid G and incenter I describe arcs, then choose C minimizing GI and maximize that minimum over alpha*

> In the plane, let $A$ and $B$ be two distinct points. For every point $C$ exterior to the line $(AB)$, denote by $G$ the isobarycenter (centroid) of triangle $ABC$ and by $I$ the center of its inscribed circle.
> \textbf{1.} Let $\alpha$ be a real number such that $0 < \alpha < \pi$. What is the set $\Gamma$ of points $C$ such that
> $$\left(\overrightarrow{CA},\ \overrightarrow{CB}\right) = \alpha + 2k\pi,$$
> $k$ being an integer? When $C$ describes $\Gamma$, show that $G$ and $I$ describe two arcs of a circle, which you will specify.
> \textbf{2.} Suppose now that $\dfrac{\pi}{3} < \alpha < \pi$. How must $C$ be chosen in $\Gamma$ so that the distance $GI$ is minimal?
> \textbf{3.} Denote by $f(\alpha)$ the minimal distance $GI$ of the previous question. Express $f(\alpha)$ in terms of $a = AB$ and $\alpha$. Determine the maximal value of $f(\alpha)$ when $\alpha$ describes $\left]\dfrac{\pi}{3};\ \pi\right[$.
