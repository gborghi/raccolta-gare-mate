*Max subset of {1,...,3n} with no x+y=z*

> It is given the set $S = \{1, 2, 3, \cdots, 3n\}$ where $n$ is a positive integer. $T$ is a subset of $S$ such that, for any $x, y, z \in T$ (where $x$, $y$, $z$ can be the same), $x + y \neq z$. Find the maximum value of the number of elements in such set.

*Count n-digit wave numbers; find f(10) and f(2008) mod 13*

> Let $a$ be an $n$-digit integer $a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n$ (a natural number) that satisfies the following conditions:
> 
> (i) $a_i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$, and $a_i \ne a_{i+1}$, $i = 1, 2, \ldots, n-1$.
> 
> (ii) When $n \ge 3$, the numbers $a_i - a_{i+1}$ and $a_{i+1} - a_{i+2}$ have opposite signs, $i = 1, 2, \ldots, n-2$.
> 
> Let $f(n)$ denote the number of such $n$-digit integers. Find
> 
> (1) the value of $f(10)$;
> 
> (2) the remainder of $f(2008)$ divided by $13$.

**Topic:** [[topic_combinatoria|Combinatoria]], [[topic_aritmetica|Aritmetica / Teoria dei Numeri]]
**Metodo:** [[method_ricorsione|Ricorsione]], [[method_congruenze|Congruenze]], [[method_induzione|Induzione]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_riconoscimento_pattern|Riconoscimento di pattern]], [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_conteggio_sistematico|Conteggio sistematico]]
**Area:** [[Combinatoria, Logica e Probabilita]], [[Aritmetica e Teoria dei Numeri]]
**Risposta:** $f(10) = 8008; f(2008) \equiv 10 \pmod{13}$
**Fonte:** [apri libro PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1A6F-Sk42BaQt80ts_sFXZiDY4uHJtL3I/view)


<span class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></span>


*Contare i numeri di onde a n cifre; trovare f(10) e f(2008) mod 13*

> $a$ è un numero intero $n$ a 2 cifre $a_1 a_2 \cdots a_n$ (un numero naturale) che soddisfa le seguenti condizioni:
> 
> (i) $a_i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$ e $a_i \ne a_{i+1}$, $i = 1, 2, \ldots, n-1$.
> 
> ii) Quando $n \ge 3$, i numeri $a_i - a_{i+1}$ e $a_{i+1} - a_{i+2}$ presentano segni opposti, $i = 1, 2, \ldots, n-2$.
> 
> $f(n)$ indichi il numero di tali integri $n$. Trova
> 
> (1) il valore di $f(10)$;
> 
> (2) il residuo di $f(2008)$ diviso per $13$.

**Risposta:** f(10) = 8008; f(2008) \equiv 10 \pmod{13}

*Captain Jack's winning strategy in gold-box selection game*

> Captain Jack and his pirates robbed 6 boxes of gold coins $A_1$, $A_2$, $A_3$, $A_4$, $A_5$, $A_6$. There are $a_i$ coins in box $A_i$ ($i = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6$) and $a_i \neq a_j$ ($i \neq j$). They laid the boxes as shown in the figure. Captain Jack would take turns with a nominated pirate to choose a box. The rule was: Each person could only choose a box which was adjacent to at most one box. If Captain Jack got more gold coins than the pirates, the Captain won the game. If Captain Jack went first, what should be his strategy to win the game?
