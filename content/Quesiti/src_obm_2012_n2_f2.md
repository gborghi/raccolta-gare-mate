*Reducing 1 to 100 by iterated operations; find last remaining number*

> Zoroastro wrote the numbers $1, 2, \ldots, 100$ in a black box. He will perform operations to reduce the quantity of numbers until only one remains. The first operation consists of choosing any two numbers $a$ and $b$ from the box and replacing them by $a + b - 1$. The second operation consists of choosing any two numbers $a$ and $b$ and replacing them by $a + b - 2$. In general, after $k$ operations the new operation is: choose any two numbers $a$ and $b$ and replace them by $a + b - (k+1)$. Determine which number will remain at the end.

*Riduzione da 1 a 100 con operazioni iterate; trovare l'ultimo numero rimanente*

> Zoroastro ha scritto i numeri $1, 2, \ldots, 100$ in una scatola nera. Eseguirà operazioni per ridurre la quantità di numeri finché non rimarrà solo uno. La prima operazione consiste nel scegliere da questa casella due numeri $a$ e $b$ e sostituirli con $a + b - 1$. In generale, dopo le operazioni $k$ la nuova operazione è: scegliere due numeri $a$ e $b$ e sostituirli da $a + b - (k+1)$. Determina quale numero rimarrà alla fine.

*Palindromes in base 10: find all two-digit ab with ab+ba and ab*ba both palindromes*

> A number is a *palindrome* when the sequence of digits reading left to right is the same as reading right to left. For example, $12321$ is a palindrome. Determine all two-digit numbers $\overline{ab}$ (in base 10) such that $\overline{ab} + \overline{ba}$ and $\overline{ab} \times \overline{ba}$ are both palindromes. For example, $12 + 21 = 33$ and $12 \times 21 = 252$ show that the number $12$ satisfies these conditions.
