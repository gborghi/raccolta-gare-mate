*Angle bisectors of triangle define three collinear intersection points via Ceva/Menelaus*

> Let $AA_1$, $BB_1$, and $CC_1$ be angular bisectors of $\triangle ABC$. Prove that points $A_3'$, $B_3'$, $C_3'$ are collinear. (posed by Tao Pingsheng)

*Prove sum of distances from n points to any point on segment AB does not exceed max of sums to endpoints*

> Given $n$ points $P_1, P_2, \ldots, P_n$ on a plane, let $M$ be any point on segment $AB$ on the plane. Denote by $|P_i M|$ the distance between $P_i$ and $M$, $i = 1, 2, 3, \ldots, n$. Prove that $$\sum_{i=1}^{n} |P_i M| \leq \max\!\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n} |P_i A|,\; \sum_{i=1}^{n} |P_i B|\right\}.$$ (posed by Jin Mengwei)

*Color 12 clock-face points in 4 colors; find largest n convex quadrilaterals with no shared color among any three*

> Consider 12 figures on the clock face as 12 points. Color them in four colors, red, yellow, blue and green. Each color is used for three points. Configure convex quadrilaterals with vertices in these points such that each quadrilateral has one vertex of each color. Find the largest number $n$ of such quadrilaterals such that among any three of these quadrilaterals there is no vertex of the same color at the same position. (posed by Tao Pingsheng)

*Colora i 12 punti del quadrante dell'orologio con 4 colori; trova i n quadrilateri convessi più grandi tali che, tra tre qualsiasi di essi, nessun colore sia condiviso*

> Si considerino 12 figure sul quadrante dell'orologio come 12 punti. Si colorino con quattro colori: rosso, giallo, blu e verde. Ogni colore viene usato per tre punti. Si costruiscano quadrilateri convessi i cui vertici siano questi punti, in modo che ciascun quadrilatero abbia un vertice di ciascun colore. Si determini il numero massimo $n$ di tali quadrilateri tale che, tra qualsiasi tre di questi quadrilateri, non vi sia alcun vertice dello stesso colore nella stessa posizione. (proposto da Tao Pingsheng)
