The sum of the numbers on all eleven visible faces is $36$. What is the sum of the numbers on the three faces that are in contact with the table?

*Two decks of 50 double-sided cards each showing 1-100; show all 100 cards can be placed so the up faces show every number from 1 to 100.*

> Isabel has two decks, each with $50$ cards. On each of the decks are written the numbers from $1$ to $100$ (on each card two numbers are written, one on each face of the card). Due to a manufacturing defect, the distribution of the numbers on the cards is not the same in the two decks (for example, in one of the decks the $1$ appears on the same card as the $2$; in the other, the $1$ appears with the $76$). Show how Isabel should proceed so that, when placing the $100$ cards on a table, the faces turned upward show all the numbers from $1$ to $100$.

*Paper-folding trisection of an angle; prove lines AA' and AB' divide angle alpha into three equal parts.*

> On a sheet of paper a line $r$ passes through the corner $A$ of the sheet and forms an angle $\alpha$ with the horizontal border, as in figure 1. To divide this angle $\alpha$ into three equal parts, we carry out the following constructions:
> a) initially, we mark two points $B$ and $C$ on the vertical border so that $AB = BC$; through the point $B$ we draw the line $s$ parallel to the border (figure 2);
> b) next, we fold the paper, adjusting it so that the point $C$ coincides with a point $C'$ on the line $r$ and the point $A$ coincides with a point $A'$ on the line $s$ (figure 3); we call $B'$ the point with which $B$ coincides.
> 
> Show that the lines $AA'$ and $AB'$ divide the angle $\alpha$ into three equal parts.

![[src_obm_2000_n2_f3__Q03.png]]

> Su un foglio di carta una linea $r$ attraversa l'angolo $A$ del foglio e forma un angolo $\alpha$ con il bordo orizzontale, come nella figura 1. Per dividere questo angolo $\alpha$ in tre parti uguali realizziamo le seguenti costruzioni: a) inizialmente, segnichiamo due punti $B$ e $C$ sul confine verticale in modo che $AB = BC$; attraverso il punto $B$ disegniamo la linea $s$ parallela al confine (figura 2); b) poi, piegiamo la carta, ajustandola in modo che il punto $C$ coincida con un punto $C'$ sulla linea $r$ e il punto $A$ coincida con un punto $A'$ sulla linea $s$ (figura 3); chiamiamo $B'$ il punto con cui $B$ coincide.
> 
> Indicare che le linee $AA'$ e $AB'$ dividono l'angolo $\alpha$ in tre parti uguali.

*Decide whether two distinct powers of 2 with the same number of digits can be digit-rearrangements of each other.*

> Is it possible to find two powers of $2$, distinct and with the same number of digits, such that one can be obtained through a rearrangement of the digits of the other?

**Metodo:** [[method_congruenze|Congruenze]], [[method_casework|Casework]]

**Area:** [[Aritmetica e Teoria dei Numeri]], [[Combinatoria, Logica e Probabilita]]
**Risposta:** No, it is not possible.
