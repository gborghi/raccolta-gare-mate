*Angle ABC = 90° iff AD = CE; points on circle with perpendicularity conditions*

> Points $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ and $E$ lie, in that order, on a circle and the lines $AB$ and $DC$ are produced to meet at $F$. The line through $A$ which is perpendicular to $AB$, the line through $D$ which is perpendicular to $DC$, and the perpendicular bisector of $BC$ are concurrent. Prove that $\angle ABC = 90^\circ$ if and only if $AD = CE$.

*Integer map (x,y)→(x²+y, x−y) with x+y=p²; count distinct treasure locations*

> Long John Silverman has a map on which the point $P$ has integer coordinates $(x, y)$ with $x > 0$, $y > 0$, and $x + y = p^2$ for some prime $p$. On the map the point $(x, y)$ is mapped to the point $(x^2 + y,\, x - y)$ where $x > y > 0$. He has indicated on the map that the three numbers $x + y$, $x^2 + y$, and $x - y$ are all distinct. Find how many different places the treasure may be hidden.

**Metodo:** [[method_congruenze|Congruenze]], [[method_casework|Casework]], [[method_fattorizzazione|Fattorizzazione]]
