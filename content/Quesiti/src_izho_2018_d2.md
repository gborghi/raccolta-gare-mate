*How many 3 by 3 questions guarantee a hit on the Crocodile's rectangle*

> The Crocodile thought of four unit squares of a $2018 \times 2018$ grid forming a rectangle with sides $1$ and $4$. The Bear can choose any square formed by $9$ unit squares ($3 \times 3$) and ask whether it contains at least one of the four Crocodile's squares. What minimum number of questions should he ask to be sure of at least one affirmative answer?

*Real a for which some f satisfies f(x-f(y)) = f(x) + a times the floor of y*

> Find all real $a$ for which there exists a function $f : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that
>
> $$f\!\left(x - f(y)\right) = f(x) + a\,\lfloor y \rfloor$$
>
> for every real $x$, $y$, where $\lfloor y \rfloor$ denotes the integral part of $y$.

**Topic:** [[topic_funzionali|Equazioni funzionali / successioni]]
**Metodo:** [[method_casework|Analisi per casi]]

*The sum of six inradii in a cyclic hexagon is at most R times square root of 3*

> A convex hexagon $ABCDEF$ is inscribed in a circle with radius $R$. Diagonals $AD$ and $BE$, $BE$ and $CF$, $AD$ and $CF$ of the hexagon meet at points $M$, $N$, $K$ respectively. Let $r_1, r_2, r_3, r_4, r_5, r_6$ be the inradii of the triangles $ABM$, $BCN$, $CDK$, $DEM$, $EFN$, $AFK$ respectively. Prove that
>
> $$r_1 + r_2 + r_3 + r_4 + r_5 + r_6 \leq R\sqrt{3}.$$

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]], [[topic_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]], [[topic_trigonometria|Trigonometry]]
**Metodo:** [[method_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze classiche]], [[method_trigonometria|Trigonometric techniques]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_ragionamento_geometrico|Ragionamento geometrico]]
**Area:** [[Geometria|Geometry]], [[Algebra e Analisi|Algebra and analysis]]
**Fonte:** apri PDF

*Somma degli inraggi di sei triangoli in un esagono ciclico, al più R per radice di 3*

> Un esagono convesso $ABCDEF$ è inscritto in una circonferenza di raggio $R$. Le diagonali $AD$ e $BE$, $BE$ e $CF$, $AD$ e $CF$ si incontrano rispettivamente in $M$, $N$, $K$. Siano $r_1, \ldots, r_6$ gli inraggi dei triangoli $ABM$, $BCN$, $CDK$, $DEM$, $EFN$, $AFK$. Dimostrare che
> $$r_1+r_2+r_3+r_4+r_5+r_6 \le R\sqrt{3}.$$
