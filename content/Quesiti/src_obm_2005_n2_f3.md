*Swimmer and log problem: time for log to travel from A to B*

> In his daily swimming training, Esmeraldinho swims several times between two points $A$ and $B$ situated on the same bank of a river, with a constant stroke rate. The current of the river flows from $A$ to $B$, and the current in the opposite direction is against Esmeraldinho. A log carried by the current passes point $A$ at the exact moment that Esmeraldinho sets off from $A$. Esmeraldinho reaches $B$ and immediately returns toward $A$. On the return trip, he crosses the log 6 minutes after leaving $A$. Esmeraldinho then reaches $A$ and immediately sets off again toward $B$, reaching $B$ 5 minutes after passing the log for the second time while going from $B$ to $A$. How many minutes does the log take to travel from $A$ to $B$?

La corrente del fiume scorre da $A$ a $B$, e la corrente nella direzione opposta è contro Esmeraldinho. Un registro trasportato dal punto di passaggio corrente $A$ al momento esatto in cui Esmeraldinho parte da $A$. Esmeraldinho raggiunge $B$ e ritorna immediatamente verso $A$. Nel viaggio di ritorno, attraversa il tronco 6 minuti dopo aver lasciato il$A$. Esmeraldinho raggiunge quindi $A$ e immediatamente parte di nuovo verso $B$, raggiungendo $B$ 5 minuti dopo aver superato il registro per la seconda volta mentre passa da $B$ a $A$. Quanti minuti ci vogliono per spostare il registro da $A$ a $B$?

*Angle bisectors and external bisector: prove angle APM = 30 degrees*

> The measure of angle $B$ of triangle $ABC$ is $120^\circ$. Let $M$ be a point on side $AC$ and $K$ a point on the extension of side $AB$ beyond $B$, such that $BM$ is the internal bisector of angle $\angle ABC$ and $CK$ is the external bisector corresponding to angle $\angle ACB$. The segment $MK$ intersects $BC$ at point $P$. Prove that $\angle APM = 30^\circ$.
