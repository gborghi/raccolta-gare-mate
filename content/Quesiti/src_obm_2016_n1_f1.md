*Evaluate (2016^2-1)/2015.*

> Compute the value of $\dfrac{2016^2-1}{2015}$.
> 
> [Source is the official answer key (GABARITO); the original multiple-choice statement and options are not reproduced. From the solution: $\dfrac{2016^2-1}{2015}=\dfrac{(2016+1)(2016-1)}{2015}=\dfrac{2017\times 2015}{2015}=2017$. Alternatively, $\dfrac{2016^2-1}{2015}=\dfrac{4064256-1}{2015}=\dfrac{4064255}{2015}=2017$.]

> [Source is the answer key; original statement/options not reproduced. *Count distinct products P from a digit puzzle with nonzero digits and Z=S^3 x I^2.*

> Each letter is a nonzero digit; count the number of distinct values the product $P = S\times E\times I\times S\times E\times Z$ can take.
> 
> [Source is the answer key; original statement/options not reproduced. From the solution: since all digits are nonzero, cancelling repeated terms gives $Z = S^3\times I^2$. As $Z$ is a digit, either $Z = 1$ or $S = 2$. In the first case $I^2 = 4$ or $I^2 = 9$; in the second case the possibilities are $(S,I) = (1,2),(1,3)$ or $(2,1)$.
