*Cyclic quadrilateral ABCD; angle bisectors meet at P and Q; prove line PQ is perpendicular to MN where M,N are arc midpoints.*

> A quadrilateral $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle $o$. The bisectors of the angles $DAB$ and $ABC$ meet at $P$, and the bisectors of the angles $BCD$ and $CDA$ meet at $Q$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the arc $BC$ of $o$ not containing $D$ and $A$, and $N$ be the midpoint of the arc $DA$ of $o$ not containing $B$ and $C$. Prove that the line through $P$ and $Q$ is perpendicular to $MN$.

*A function f on pairs of nonnegative integers satisfies given recurrences; given f(a,b)=n, find the number of integral solutions x of f(a,x)+f(b,x)=n.*

> A function $f$ from the pairs of nonnegative integers to the real numbers satisfies the following conditions:
> $$f(0,0) = 0, \qquad f(2x,2y) = f(2x+1,2y+1) = f(x,y),$$
> $$f(2x+1,2y) = f(2x,2y+1) = f(x,y) + 1$$
> for all nonnegative integers $x, y$. Let $n$ and $a, b$ be nonnegative integers such that $f(a,b) = n$. Find the number of integral solutions $x$ of the equation $f(a,x) + f(b,x) = n$.

> Una funzione $f$ dalle coppie di integri non negativi ai numeri reali soddisfa le seguenti condizioni: $$f(0,0) = 0, \qquad f(2x,2y) = f(2x+1,2y+1) = f(x,y),$$ $$f(2x+1,2y) = f(2x,2y+1) = f(x,y) + 1$$ per tutti gli integri non negativi $x, y$. $n$ e $a, b$ siano integri non negativi come $f(a,b) = n$. Trova il numero di soluzioni integrali $x$ dell'equazione $f(a,x) + f(b,x) = n$.
