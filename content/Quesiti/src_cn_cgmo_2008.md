*Equilateral triangles on convex quad sides; maximize ratio y/x*

> Outside a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ we construct equilateral triangles $ABQ$, $BCR$, $CDS$ and $DAP$. Denoting by $x$ the sum of the diagonals of $ABCD$, and by $y$ the sum of line segments joining the midpoints of opposite sides of $PQRS$, we find the maximum value of $\dfrac{y}{x}$. (Posed by Xiong Bin)

*Sequence of positive numbers with recurrence; find monotonicity threshold a*

> Suppose that the sequence of positive numbers $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n, \ldots$ satisfies $(8x_2 - 7x_1)x_1^7 = 8$ and
> $$x_{k+1}x_{k-1} - x_k^2 = \frac{x_{k-1}^8 - x_k^8}{x_k^7 x_{k-1}^7}, \quad k \ge 2.$$
> Find positive real number $a$ such that when $x_1 > a$ one has $x_1 > x_2 > \cdots > x_n > \cdots$, and when $0 < x_1 < a$ one does not have such monotonicity. (Posed by Li Shenghong)

**Topic:** [[topic_algebra|Algebra]], [[topic_insiemi_funzioni|Insiemi e funzioni]], [[topic_funzionali|Functional equations / sequences]]
**Metodo:** [[method_induzione|Induzione]], [[method_ricorsione|Ricorsione]], [[method_estremalita|Estremalità]], [[method_fattorizzazione|Factorisation / algebraic manipulation]]

**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi|Algebra and analysis]]
**Risposta:** $a = 8^{1/8}$
**Fonte:** [apri libro PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1WZGXm4nA5dcZd9asSbnzGZkeWDSjqbF6/view)

> [!warning] Estratto da verificare con la fonte.
