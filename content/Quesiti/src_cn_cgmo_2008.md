*Divide sets into 3-element subsets with equal element sums*

> (1) Can one divide the set $\{1, 2, \ldots, 96\}$ into 32 subsets, each containing three elements, and the sums of the three elements in each subset are all equal?
> 
> (2) Can one divide the set $\{1, 2, \ldots, 99\}$ into 33 subsets, each containing three elements, and the sums of the three elements in each subset are all equal? (Posed by Liu Shixiong)

**Area:** [[Combinatoria, Logica e Probabilita]], [[Aritmetica e Teoria dei Numeri]]
**Risposta:** (1) No; (2) Yes

*Find smallest a>1 for triangle area ratios inside a square*

*Equilateral triangles on convex quad sides; maximize ratio y/x*

> Outside a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ we construct equilateral triangles $ABQ$, $BCR$, $CDS$ and $DAP$. (Posed by Xiong Bin)

*Sequence of positive numbers with recurrence; find monotonicity threshold a*

> Suppose that the sequence of positive numbers $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n, \ldots$ satisfies $(8x_2 - 7x_1)x_1^7 = 8$ and
> $$x_{k+1}x_{k-1} - x_k^2 = \frac{x_{k-1}^8 - x_k^8}{x_k^7 x_{k-1}^7}, \quad k \ge 2.$$
> Find positive real number $a$ such that when $x_1 > a$ one has $x_1 > x_2 > \cdots > x_n > \cdots$, and when $0 < x_1 < a$ one does not have such monotonicity. (Posed by Li Shenghong)

**Topic:** [[topic_algebra|Algebra]], [[topic_insiemi_funzioni|Insiemi e funzioni]]
**Metodo:** [[method_induzione|Induzione]], [[method_ricorsione|Ricorsione]], [[method_estremalita|Estremalità]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_riconoscimento_pattern|Riconoscimento di pattern]], [[skill_lettura_attenta|Lettura attenta]]
**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi]]
**Risposta:** $a = 8^{1/8}$
**Fonte:** [apri libro PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1WZGXm4nA5dcZd9asSbnzGZkeWDSjqbF6/view)

> [!warning] Estratto da verificare con la fonte.

*Count harmonic 2008x2008 chessboards filled with four letters*

> For a chessboard of the size $2008 \times 2008$, in each case (they all have different colors) write one of the letters $C$, $G$, $M$, $O$. If every $2 \times 2$ square contains all these four letters, we call it a "harmonic chessboard." How many different harmonic chessboards are there? (Posed by Zuming Feng)

**Risposta:** $12 \times 2^{2008} - 24$
**Fonte:** [apri libro PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1WZGXm4nA5dcZd9asSbnzGZkeWDSjqbF6/view)
