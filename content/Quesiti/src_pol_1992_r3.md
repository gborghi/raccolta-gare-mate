*Find all functions on rationals with two functional equations*

*Solve f_n(x)=2x for recursive square-root sequence*

> The sequence of functions $f_n : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is defined by $f_0(x) = 8$ and
> $$f_{n+1}(x) = \sqrt{x^2 + 6f_n(x)} \quad \text{for all } x.$$
> For every integer $n \ge 0$, solve the equation $f_n(x) = 2x$.

**Metodo:** [[method_ricorsione|Ricorsione]], [[method_induzione|Induzione]]

**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1y0mu5QcC1ogDOJ92TBWc65WHpU-0WUHz/view)

*Equal sums of odd/even lateral edge segments on regular 2n-gon pyramid*

> The base of a regular pyramid is a regular $2n$-gon $A_1 A_2 \ldots A_{2n}$. A sphere passing through the top vertex $S$ intersects the lateral edge $SA_i$ at $B_i$ for $i = 1, 2, \ldots, 2n$. Prove that $\sum_{i=1}^{n} SB_{2i-1} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} SB_{2i}$.

*Summe uguali di segmenti di bordi laterali pari/pari su piramide regolare a 2 n-gon*

> La base di una piramide regolare è un $2n$-gon $A_1 A_2 \ldots A_{2n}$ regolare. Una sfera che passa attraverso la vertica superiore $S$ incrocia il bordo laterale $SA_i$ a $B_i$ per $i = 1, 2, \ldots, 2n$. Prove che $\sum_{i=1}^{n} SB_{2i-1} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} SB_{2i}$.
