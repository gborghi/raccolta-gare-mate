*Find all non-negative c with d(n)+p(n)=n+c for some positive integer n*

> Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$ (the set of positive integers), and $d(n)$ be the number of positive divisors of $n$. Next, $p(n)$ denotes the number of integers in the closed interval $[1, n]$ which are co-prime with $n$. Find all non-negative integers $c$ such that there exists $n \in \mathbb{N}$ satisfying $d(n) + p(n) = n + c$.

**Topic:** [[topic_aritmetica|Aritmetica / Teoria dei Numeri]]
**Metodo:** [[method_casework|Casework]], [[method_congruenze|Congruenze]], [[method_fattorizzazione|Factorisation / algebraic manipulation]]

**Area:** [[Aritmetica e Teoria dei Numeri|Arithmetic and number theory]]
**Fonte:** [apri libro PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Bie5BlTQQyjdGifDyOeZ-kZzNFzUwbxT/view)

Trovare tutti gli interi non negativi $c$ tali che esista $n \in \mathbb{N}$ che soddisfi $d(n) + p(n) = n + c$.

*Determine parity of good couples S in a 2-colored m×n chessboard*

> All the grids of an $m \times n$ chessboard ($m \ge 3$, $n \ge 3$) are colored either red or blue. Two adjacent grids (with a common side) are called a good couple if they are of different colors. Suppose that there are $S$ good couples, explain how to determine whether $S$ is odd or even. Does $S$ depend on certain specific color grids?

*Prove 2(AF+BD+CE)=l iff P lies on incenter-circumcenter line in acute triangle*

> Let $l$ be the perimeter of an acute triangle $\triangle ABC$ which is not equilateral. $P$ is a variable point inside $\triangle ABC$, and $D$, $E$ and $F$ are the projections of $P$ on $BC$, $CA$ and $AB$ respectively. Prove that $2(AF + BD + CE) = l$ if and only if $P$ is collinear with the incenter and circumcenter of $\triangle ABC$.

![[src_cn_cwmo_2004__Q07.png]]

**Area:** [[Geometria]]
**Fonte:** [apri libro PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Bie5BlTQQyjdGifDyOeZ-kZzNFzUwbxT/view)

*2(AF+BD+CE)=l se e solo se P giace sulla retta che congiunge incentro e circocentro in un triangolo acutangolo*

Dimostrare che $2(AF + BD + CE) = l$ se e solo se $P$ è allineato con l'incentro e il circocentro di $\triangle ABC$.
