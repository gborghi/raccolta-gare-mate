*Incircle touch points; line PQ bisects two sides*

> The in-circle of triangle $ABC$ touches the sides $BC$, $CA$ and $AB$ in $K$, $L$ and $M$ respectively. The line through $A$ and parallel to $LK$ meets $MK$ in $P$ and the line through $A$ and parallel to $MK$ meets $LK$ in $Q$. Show that the line $PQ$ bisects the sides $AB$ and $AC$ of triangle $ABC$.

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]]
**Metodo:** [[method_simmetria|Simmetria]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_ragionamento_geometrico|Ragionamento geometrico]], [[skill_lettura_attenta|Lettura attenta]]
**Area:** [[Geometria]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/19gclPYoes6dSVvxjkTBtcJvWFswaMdDg/view)


<span class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></span>


*Punti di contatto in cerchio; la linea PQ divide due lati*

> Il cerchio del triangolo $ABC$ tocca rispettivamente i lati $BC$, $CA$ e $AB$ di $K$, $L$ e $M$. La linea attraverso $A$ e parallela a $LK$ incontra $MK$ in $P$ e la linea attraverso $A$ e parallela a $MK$ incontra $LK$ in $Q$. Indicare che la linea $PQ$ divide i lati $AB$ e $AC$ del triangolo $ABC$.

*Equal ratios with abc nonzero: a+b+c=0 or a=b=c*

> If $a, b, c$ are real numbers such that $abc \neq 0$ and
> $$\frac{xb + (1-x)c}{a} = \frac{xc + (1-x)a}{b} = \frac{xa + (1-x)b}{c},$$
> then prove that either $a + b + c = 0$ or $a = b = c$.

**Metodo:** [[method_simmetria|Simmetria]], [[method_fattorizzazione|Fattorizzazione]]

*Rationi uguali con abc non zero: a+b+c=0 o a=b=c*

*Convex quadrilateral with special sides and angle; prove angle difference 90°*

> In a convex quadrilateral $PQRS$, $PQ = RS = (\sqrt{3}+1)QR = SP$ and $\angle RSP - \angle SPQ = 30^\circ$. Prove that
> $$\angle PQR - \angle QRS = 90^\circ.$$

<span class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></span>


*Quadrilatero convex con lati e angoli speciali; prova la differenza di angolo 90°*

> In un quadrilaterale converso $PQRS$, $PQ = RS = (\sqrt{3}+1)QR = SP$ e $\angle RSP - \angle SPQ = 30^\circ$. Provare che $$\angle PQR - \angle QRS = 90^\circ.$$

*f(n) counts integer-sided triangles; prove f(1999)>f(1996) and f(2000)=f(1997)*

> For any natural number $n$ ($n \ge 3$), let $f(n)$ denote the number of non-congruent integer-sided triangles with perimeter $n$ (e.g., $f(3) = 1$, $f(4) = 0$, $f(7) = 2$). Show that
> (a) $f(1999) > f(1996)$;
> (b) $f(2000) = f(1997)$.
