*Reflection of midpoint over line DN lies inside triangle, AM' perp BC*

> In triangle $ABC$ with $AB < AC$, let $M$ be the midpoint of side $BC$. Let $N$ be the midpoint of the arc $BC$ not containing $A$ on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$. Let $D$ be the intersection of the angle bisector of $\angle BAC$ with side $BC$. Let $M'$ be the reflection of $M$ over line $DN$. Prove that $M'$ lies in the interior of triangle $ABC$ (not on its boundary), and that line $AM'$ is perpendicular to line $BC$. Here, $XY$ denotes the length of segment $XY$.

*Maximum number of white cells in 2024×2024 colored grid*

> A $2024 \times 2024$ grid is given. Each cell is colored red, blue, or white, with every cell receiving exactly one color. The coloring satisfies the following condition: each red cell has exactly $1$ red cell among its (at most $4$) neighbors sharing a side; and for every $2 \times 2$ square of cells, the number of blue cells it contains is either $1$ or $2$. Find the maximum possible number of white cells.

*Numero massimo di celle bianche nella griglia colorata 2024×2024*

> È data una griglia $2024 \times 2024$. Ogni cella è colorata di rosso, di blu o di bianco, e ogni cella riceve esattamente un colore. La colorazione soddisfa la seguente condizione: ogni cella rossa ha esattamente $1$ celle rosse tra i suoi vicini (al massimo $4$) che condividono un lato; e, per ogni quadrato $2 \times 2$ di celle, il numero di celle blu che esso contiene è oppure $1$ oppure $2$. Determinare il numero massimo possibile di celle bianche.

*Similarity of triangles B'C'D' and BCD in acute triangle*

> In acute triangle $ABC$, let $D$ be the point on side $BC$ satisfying $AB : AC = DB : BC$. Let $B'$ be the reflection of $B$ over line $AC$, let $C'$ be the reflection of $C$ over line $AB$, and let $D'$ be the reflection of $D$ over the perpendicular bisector of $BC$. Prove that triangle $B'C'D'$ is similar to triangle $BCD$. Here, $XY$ denotes the length of segment $XY$.
