*Fibonacci sequence divisibility by m for all n*

> The Fibonacci sequence $\{f_n\}$ is defined by
> $$f_1 = 1, \quad f_2 = 1, \quad f_n = f_{n-1} + f_{n-2} \quad (n \ge 2).$$
> Prove that there are unique integers $a$, $b$, $m$ such that $0 < a < m$, $0 < b < m$ and $f_n - ab^n$ is divisible by $m$ for all positive integers $n$.

*Sum and product of reciprocals of iterated sequence satisfy identity*

> The real numbers $x_1, x_2, x_3, \ldots$ are defined by
> $$x_1 = x, \quad x_{n+1} = x_n^2 + 1 \quad \text{for all } n \ge 1.$$
> $S_n$ is the sum and $P_n$ is the product of the first $n$ terms of the sequence $\gamma_1, \gamma_2, \gamma_3, \ldots$ where $\gamma_n = \frac{1}{x_n}$. Show that $x S_n + P_n = 1$ for all $n$.

*Shortest path from dome high point to cylinder base corner*

> The two cylindrical surfaces
> $$x^2 + y^2 = 1, \quad |y| \le a, \quad x > 0$$
> and
> $$y^2 + z^2 = 1, \quad x > 0, \quad |x| \le a$$
> intersect and together with the plane $z = 0$ enclose a dome-like shape which is here called a "cupola". The cupola is placed on top of a vertical tower of height $h$ whose horizontal cross-section is a square of side $2a$. Find the shortest distance from the highest point of the cupola to a corner of the base of the tower, over the surface of the cupola and tower.

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_solida|Geometria solida]], [[topic_geometria_analitica|Coordinate geometry]], [[topic_geometria_piana|Plane geometry]]
**Metodo:** [[method_coordinate|Coordinate]], [[method_estremalita|Estremalità]]

**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/13blbg3MFhT-UVgIheFrX8WBP_TBbhjZm/view)

*Percorso più breve dal punto più alto della cupola a un angolo della base della torre*

> Le due superfici cilindriche
> $$x^2 + y^2 = 1, \quad |y| \le a, \quad x > 0$$
> e
> $$y^2 + z^2 = 1, \quad x > 0, \quad |x| \le a$$
> si intersecano e, insieme al piano $z = 0$, racchiudono una forma a cupola che qui viene chiamata "cupola". La cupola è posta sopra una torre verticale di altezza $h$ la cui sezione orizzontale è un quadrato di lato $2a$. Trovare la distanza minima dal punto più alto della cupola a un angolo della base della torre, lungo la superficie della cupola e della torre.

*Square-root equation: find p giving exactly one real solution*

> Consider the equation
> $$\sqrt{2p + 1 - x^2} + \sqrt{3x + p + 4} = \sqrt{x^2 + 9x + 3p + 9} \tag{1}$$
> in which $x, p$ are real numbers and the square roots are to be real and non-negative. Show that if (1) holds then
> $$(x^2 + x - p)(x^2 + 8x + 2p + 9) = 0.$$
> Hence find the set of real numbers $p$ for which (1) is satisfied by exactly one real number $x$.
