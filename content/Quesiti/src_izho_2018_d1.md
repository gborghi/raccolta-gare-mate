*An inequality between the cosines of the angles and the sides of a triangle*

> Let $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$ be the angles of a triangle opposite to the sides $a$, $b$, $c$ respectively. Prove the inequality
>
> $$
> 2\!\left(\cos^2\alpha + \cos^2\beta + \cos^2\gamma\right) \geq \frac{a^2}{b^2+c^2} + \frac{b^2}{a^2+c^2} + \frac{c^2}{a^2+b^2}.
> $$

**Metodo:** [[method_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze classiche]], [[method_trigonometria|Trigonometric techniques]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]]
**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi|Algebra and analysis]], [[Geometria|Geometry]]

*The incenters of APL and BPK give IP = JQ along the bisector*

> Points $N$, $K$, $L$ lie on the sides $AB$, $BC$, $CA$ of a triangle $ABC$ respectively, so that $AL = BK$ and $CN$ is the bisector of the angle $C$. The segments $AK$ and $BL$ meet at the point $P$. Let $I$ and $J$ be the incentres of the triangles $APL$ and $BPK$ respectively. The lines $CN$ and $IJ$ meet at point $Q$. Prove that $IP = JQ$.

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]]
**Metodo:** [[method_simmetria|Sfruttamento della simmetria]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_ragionamento_geometrico|Ragionamento geometrico]]
**Area:** [[Geometria]]
**Fonte:** apri PDF


<div class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></div>


*Gli incentri di APL e BPK danno IP = JQ sulla bisettrice*

Siano $I$ e $J$ gli incentri dei triangoli $APL$ e $BPK$. Le rette $CN$ e $IJ$ si incontrano in $Q$. Dimostrare che $IP = JQ$.

*Infinitely many positive pairs for which m+n divides (m!)^n + (n!)^m + 1*

> Prove that there exist infinitely many pairs $(m, n)$ of positive integers such that
>
> $$
> m + n \;\Big|\; (m!)^n + (n!)^m + 1.
> $$
