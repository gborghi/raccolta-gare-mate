*Practice-match outcomes giving exactly 2 possible champions*

> $2^3 = 8$ players take part in a single-elimination chess tournament, in which the champion is decided as follows. First, all players are lined up in a row. Then the following operation is repeated $3$ times: the players in the row are grouped into pairs, two at a time from one end, and the two players in each pair play a match; the winner stays in the row and the loser drops out. (There are no draws.) The player remaining in the row at the end is the champion. Before the tournament a full round-robin of practice matches was held, in which no draws occurred; that is, for every pair of players a practice match was played and a winner determined. Now assume the results of the tournament matches agree with the practice-match results. Depending on the initial lineup of the players, the number of players who could possibly become champion turned out to be exactly $2$. How many possible combinations of the practice-match results are there (for which exactly $2$ players can possibly become champion)?

*Count integers equal to sum of base-7 single-digit-deletions*

> How many positive integers $n$ satisfy the following condition? Condition: Write $n$ in base $7$ (with nonzero leading digit) and let $k$ be its number of digits; require $k \ge 2$. After writing $n$ in base $7$, for each $i = 1, 2, \ldots, k-1$ remove the $i$-th digit from the right (the bottom), obtaining a $(k-1)$-digit base-$7$ integer $n_i$. Then $$\sum_{i=1}^{k-1} n_i = n.$$

*Conteggio di enti interi pari alla somma delle delezioni a 7 cifre di base*

> Quanti integri positivi $n$ soddisfano la seguente condizione? Condizione: Scrivere $n$ in base $7$ (con cifre di primo piano non zero) e lasciare $k$ essere il suo numero di cifre; richiedere $k \ge 2$. Dopo aver scritto $n$ nella base $7$, per ogni $i = 1, 2, \ldots, k-1$ togliere la $i$-esima cifra dalla destra (in basso), ottenendo un $(k-1)$-digito base-$7$ intero $n_i$. Poi $$\sum_{i=1}^{k-1} n_i = n.$$

*N più piccolo con a_{N+1}-a_N non uguale a 1*

> Una sequenza a valore intero $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ soddisfa, per tutti gli enti $m, n$: se $m, n \ge 30$ e $|m - n| \ge 2018$, allora $a_{m+n}$ è uguale a $a_m + n$ o $a_n + m$. Trova il più grande valore possibile di un intero positivo $N$ per il quale $a_{N+1} - a_N \ne 1$.
