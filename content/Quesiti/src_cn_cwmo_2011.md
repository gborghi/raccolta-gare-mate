*Maximum size of subset of {1,...,2011} with divisibility chain property*

> Let $M \subseteq \{1, 2, \ldots, 2011\}$ be a subset satisfying the following condition. For any three elements in $M$, there exist two of them $a$ and $b$, such that $a \mid b$ or $b \mid a$. Determine, with proof, the maximum value of $|M|$, where $|M|$ denotes the number of elements of $M$.

*Arrange all subsets in a sequence and compute a sum involving subset sums*

> Let $n \ge 2$ be a given integer.
> 
> (1) Prove that one can arrange all the subsets of the set $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$ as a sequence of subsets $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_{2^n}$, such that $|A_{i+1}| = |A_i| + 1$ or $|A_{i+1}| = |A_i| - 1$, where $i = 1, 2, \ldots, 2^n$ and $A_{2^n+1} = A_1$.
> 
> (2) Determine, with proof, all possible values of the sum $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{2^n} (-1)^{|S|} S(A_i)$, where $S(A_i) = \displaystyle\sum_{x \in A_i} x$ and $S(\emptyset) = 0$ for any subset sequence $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_{2^n}$ satisfying the condition in (1).

**Metodo:** [[method_induzione|Induzione]], [[method_doppio_conteggio|Doppio conteggio]], [[method_invarianti|Invarianti]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_astrazione|Astrazione]], [[skill_conteggio_sistematico|Conteggio sistematico]], [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]]
**Area:** [[Combinatoria, Logica e Probabilita]], [[Algebra e Analisi]]

*Disponi tutti i sottoinsiemi in una sequenza e calcola una somma che coinvolga le somme dei sottoinsiemi*

> Sia $n \ge 2$ un intero dato.
>
> (1) Dimostrare che si possono disporre tutti i sottoinsiemi dell'insieme $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$ in una successione di sottoinsiemi $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_{2^n}$, tale che $|A_{i+1}| = |A_i| + 1$ oppure $|A_{i+1}| = |A_i| - 1$, dove $i = 1, 2, \ldots, 2^n$ e $A_{2^n+1} = A_1$.
>
> (2) Determinare, con dimostrazione, tutti i valori possibili della somma $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{2^n} (-1)^{|S|} S(A_i)$, dove $S(A_i) = \displaystyle\sum_{x \in A_i} x$ e $S(\emptyset) = 0$ per qualsiasi successione di sottoinsiemi $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_{2^n}$ che soddisfi la condizione di (1).

*Circle with perpendicular chords, inner tangent circle; prove a tangency*

> As shown in the figure, $AB$ and $CD$ are two chords in the circle $\odot O$, meeting at point $E$, and $AB \perp CD$. $\odot I$ is tangent to $\odot O$ internally at point $F$, and is tangent to the chords $AB$ and $CD$ at points $G$ and $H$, respectively. $l$ is a line passing through $O$, meeting $AB$, $CD$ at points $P$, $Q$, respectively, such that $EP = EQ$. Line $l$ meets the line $EF$ at point $M$. Prove that the line through $M$ and parallel to the line $AB$ is tangent to the circle $\odot O$.
