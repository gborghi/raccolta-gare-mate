*Chord, midpoint, tangent circle, and distance relation*

> Let $AB$ be a chord of a circle $\mathscr{C}_1$ that is not a diameter, and $M$ be the midpoint of $AB$. Let $T$ be a point on the circle $\mathscr{C}_2$ with $OM$ as diameter. The tangent to $\mathscr{C}_2$ at $T$ meets $\mathscr{C}_1$ at $P$. Show that
> $$PA^2 + PB^2 = 4PT^2.$$

*Real quadratic with complex roots implies triangle inequality*

> Suppose $a, b, c$ are real numbers such that the quadratic equation
> $$x^2 - (a + b + c)x + (ab + bc + ca) = 0$$
> has roots of the form $\alpha \pm i\beta$, where $\alpha > 0$ and $\beta \neq 0$ are real numbers. Show that:
> (a) The numbers $a, b, c$ are all positive.
> (b) The numbers $\sqrt{a}$, $\sqrt{b}$, $\sqrt{c}$ are the sides of a triangle.

**Topic:** [[topic_algebra|Algebra]], [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]], [[topic_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]]
**Metodo:** [[method_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]], [[method_casework|Casework]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_ragionamento_geometrico|Ragionamento geometrico]], [[skill_lettura_attenta|Lettura attenta]]
**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi]], [[Geometria]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1uAxelJ4GAxypZpM2ox-8kZZSTZuFphZT/view)

> abbia radici della forma $\alpha \pm i\beta$, dove $\alpha > 0$ e $\beta \neq 0$ sono numeri reali. Dimostrare che:
> (a) I numeri $a, b, c$ sono tutti positivi.
> (b) I numeri $\sqrt{a}$, $\sqrt{b}$, $\sqrt{c}$ sono i lati di un triangolo.

> We want to choose $n$ of the $2n$ integers $0, 1, 2, \ldots, 2n-1$ such that the average of the $n$ chosen integers is an integer and as small as possible. Show that this can be done for each positive integer $n$ and find this smallest value.
