*Convex pentagon ABCDE with DC=DE, angle DCB=angle DEA=90 deg; F on AB with AF:BF=AE:BC; prove two angle equalities.*

> $$\angle FCE = \angle ADE \quad \text{and} \quad \angle FEC = \angle BDC.$$
