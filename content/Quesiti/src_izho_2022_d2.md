*Infinitely many positive integers that are not a sum of consecutive polynomial values*

> A polynomial $f(x)$ with real coefficients, of degree greater than $1$, is given. Prove that there are infinitely many positive integers which cannot be written as
> $$f(n+1)+f(n+2)+\cdots+f(n+k)$$
> where $n$ and $k$ are positive integers.

**Topic:** [[topic_algebra|Algebra]]
**Metodo:** [[method_induzione|Induzione]], [[method_disuguaglianze|Classical inequalities]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_stima|Estimation and approximation]]
**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi|Algebra and analysis]]
**Fonte:** apri PDF

*Two bounded sequences that separate at least like one over square root of n*

> Do there exist two bounded sequences $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ and $b_1, b_2, \ldots$ such that for all positive integers $n$ and $m>n$ at least one of the inequalities
> $$|a_m-a_n| > \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}, \qquad |b_m-b_n| > \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}$$
> holds?

**Topic:** [[topic_funzionali|Equazioni funzionali / successioni]]
**Metodo:** [[method_estremalita|Principio di estremalita]], [[method_cassetti|Pigeonhole principle]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_astrazione|Astrazione / generalizzazione]], [[skill_stima|Estimation and approximation]]
**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi|Algebra and analysis]]
**Fonte:** apri PDF
