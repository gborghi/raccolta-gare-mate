*Find smallest k for lcm+hcf=p^k equation in positive integers*

> For any two positive integers $m$ and $n$, we define $l(m,n)$ as their least common multiple and $h(m,n)$ as their highest common factor. Given a prime $p > 3$, let $k$ denote the number of ordered pairs of positive integers $(m, n)$ satisfying the equation
> $$l(m, n) + h(m, n) = p^k.$$
> Determine the smallest value of $k$ across all choices of the prime $p > 3$.

*Convex quadrilateral ABCD: prove AC=BD iff XY bisects angle DXC*

> The convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ has sides $AD$ and $BC$ not parallel. The diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at $X$. The perpendicular bisectors of sides $AD$ and $BC$ meet at $Y$. Suppose that $Y$ lies strictly inside triangle $XCD$.
> 
> Prove that $AC = BD$ if and only if $XY$ bisects $\angle DXC$.

*Infinite sequence from partial sums: finitely many positive or finitely many negative terms?*

> Let $N$ be a positive integer and let $(k_n)_{n \geq 1}$ be a sequence of positive integers with all terms at most $N$. Annabel starts this by choosing integers $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_N$. She then extends this to an infinite sequence $(x_n)_{n \geq 1}$ of integers by defining
> $$x_n = \sum_{i=n-k_{n-1}}^{n-1} x_i$$
> for each $n > N$.
> 
> Show that there are either finitely many strictly positive terms or finitely many strictly negative terms in the infinite sequence $(x_n)$.

*Successione infinita ricavata dalle somme parziali: un numero finito di termini positivi o un numero finito di termini negativi?*

> Sia $N$ un numero intero positivo e sia $(k_n)_{n \geq 1}$ una successione di numeri interi positivi i cui termini sono tutti al più $N$. Annabel inizia scegliendo numeri interi $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_N$. Successivamente estende ciò a una successione infinita $(x_n)_{n \geq 1}$ di numeri interi definendo
> $$x_n = \sum_{i=n-k_{n-1}}^{n-1} x_i$$
> per ogni $n > N$.
>
> Dimostrare che nella successione infinita $(x_n)$ ci sono o un numero finito di termini strettamente positivi oppure un numero finito di termini strettamente negativi.
