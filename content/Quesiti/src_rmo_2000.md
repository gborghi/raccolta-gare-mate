*Construct isosceles triangles with 120-degree apex, show PQR equilateral*

> Let $AC$ be a line segment in the plane and $B$ a point between $A$ and $C$. Construct isosceles triangles $PAB$ and $QBC$ on one side of the segment $AC$ such that $\angle APB = \angle BQC = 120^\circ$, and an isosceles triangle $RAC$ on the other side of $AC$ such that $\angle ARC = 120^\circ$. Show that $PQR$ is an equilateral triangle.

*Decreasing sequence with bounded partial sums; prove sum of 1/k terms < 3*

> Suppose $(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n, \ldots)$ is a sequence of positive real numbers such that $x_1 \ge x_2 \ge x_3 \ge \cdots \ge x_n \cdots$, and for all $n$
> $$\frac{x_1}{1} + \frac{x_4}{2} + \frac{x_9}{3} + \cdots + \frac{x_{n^2}}{n} < 1.$$
> Show that for all $k$ the following inequality is satisfied:
> $$\frac{x_1}{1} + \frac{x_2}{2} + \frac{x_3}{3} + \cdots + \frac{x_k}{k} < 3.$$

**Metodo:** [[method_telescoping|Telescoping]], [[method_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]]

**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12rUdRsFDdXuqpCuHRpFpmWSAWCuy3oU3/view)

*Successione decrescente di reali positivi, con una somma parziale sui quadrati minore di 1: la somma armonica pesata è minore di 3*

> Si supponga che $(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n, \ldots)$ sia una successione di numeri reali positivi tale che $x_1 \ge x_2 \ge x_3 \ge \cdots \ge x_n \cdots$, e che per ogni $n$
>
> $$\frac{x_1}{1} + \frac{x_4}{2} + \frac{x_9}{3} + \cdots + \frac{x_{n^2}}{n} < 1.$$
>
> Dimostrare che per ogni $k$ vale la disuguaglianza
>
> $$\frac{x_1}{1} + \frac{x_2}{2} + \frac{x_3}{3} + \cdots + \frac{x_k}{k} < 3.$$
