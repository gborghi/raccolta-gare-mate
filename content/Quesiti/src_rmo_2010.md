*Geometric mean areas in concurrent-diagonal hexagon*

> Let $ABCDEF$ be a convex hexagon in which the diagonals $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ are concurrent at $O$. Suppose the area of triangle $OAF$ is the geometric mean of those of $OAB$ and $OEF$; and the area of triangle $OBC$ is the geometric mean of those of $OAB$ and $OCD$. Prove that the area of triangle $OED$ is the geometric mean of those of $OCD$ and $OEF$.

*Aree della media geometrica nell'esagono diagonale concorrente*

> Sia $ABCDEF$ un esagono convesso in cui le diagonali $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ sono concorrenti in $O$. Si supponga che l'area del triangolo $OAF$ sia la media geometrica delle aree di $OAB$ e $OEF$; e che l'area del triangolo $OBC$ sia la media geometrica delle aree di $OAB$ e $OCD$. Dimostrare che l'area del triangolo $OED$ è la media geometrica delle aree di $OCD$ e $OEF$.

*Three quadratics equal at a point implies coefficients equal*

> Let $P_1(x) = ax^2 - bx - c$, $P_2(x) = bx^2 - cx - a$, $P_3(x) = cx^2 - ax - b$ be three quadratic polynomials where $a, b, c$ are non-zero real numbers. Suppose there exists a real number $\alpha$ such that $P_1(\alpha) = P_2(\alpha) = P_3(\alpha)$. Prove that $a = b = c$.

*Count n in {1,...,2010} where floor(n/floor(sqrt(n))) equals floor((n+1)/floor(sqrt(n+1)))*
