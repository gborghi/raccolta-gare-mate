*Sum of products of all 99-element subsets of {1,...,2010} is divisible by 2011*

> For each 99-element subset $A = \{a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{99}\}$ of $\{1, 2, \ldots, 2010\}$, denote $P(A) = a_1 \cdot a_2 \cdots a_{99}$. Let $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n$ be all 99-element subsets of $\{1, 2, \ldots, 2010\}$, $n = \binom{2010}{99}$. Prove that $2011 \mid \sum_{i=1}^{n} P(A_i)$.

<span class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></span>


*La somma dei prodotti di tutti i sottoinsiemi di 99 elementi di {1,...,2010} è divisibile per 2011*

> Per ogni sottoinsieme di 99 elementi $A = \{a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{99}\}$ di $\{1, 2, \ldots, 2010\}$, indicare $P(A) = a_1 \cdot a_2 \cdots a_{99}$. $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n$ siano tutti i sottoinsiemi di 99 elementi di $\{1, 2, \ldots, 2010\}$, $n = \binom{2010}{99}$. Prove che $2011 \mid \sum_{i=1}^{n} P(A_i)$.

*Count good pairs (a,b) with 1<=a<=b<=10 and ab | a^k - b^k for some k*

*Right triangle with midpoint of two interior points; prove harmonic-mean inequality for ratios*

> As shown in Fig. 5.1. Let $C$ be the right angle of $\triangle ABC$. Let $M_1$ and $M_2$ be two arbitrary points inside $\triangle ABC$, and let $M$ be the midpoint of $M_1 M_2$. The extensions of $BM_1$, $BM_2$, and $BM$ intersect $AC$ at $N_1$, $N_2$, and $N$, respectively. Prove that $$\frac{M_1 N_1}{BM_1} + \frac{M_2 N_2}{BM_2} \ge \frac{MN}{BM}.$$

*Prove recursive formula for sequence defined by unit-fraction constraint*

> Let $\mathbf{N}^*$ be the set of positive integers. Define $a_1 = 2$, and for $n = 1, 2, \ldots,$
> $$a_{n+1} = \min\left\{\lambda \,\middle|\, \frac{1}{a_1} + \frac{1}{a_2} + \cdots + \frac{1}{a_n} + \frac{1}{\lambda} < 1,\ \lambda \in \mathbf{N}^*\right\}.$$
> Prove that $a_{n+1} = a_n^2 - a_n + 1$ for $n = 1, 2, \ldots.$

*Double sum with min of sorted sequences is non-negative*

> Let $n$ be a positive integer. The real numbers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ and $r_1, r_2, \ldots, r_n$ are such that $a_1 \le a_2 \le \cdots \le a_n$ and $0 \le r_1 \le r_2 \le \cdots \le r_n$. Prove that $$\sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{j=1}^{n} a_i a_j \min(r_i, r_j) \ge 0.$$

**Topic:** [[topic_algebra|Algebra]], [[topic_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]]
**Metodo:** [[method_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]], [[method_telescoping|Telescoping]], [[method_doppio_conteggio|Doppio conteggio]]

*Doppia somma con min delle sequenze ordinate non è negativa*
