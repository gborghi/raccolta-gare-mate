*Minimum n boxes with 10-color balls and equal color sums*

> There are $n$ boxes, each containing one ball painted in one color. The balls use exactly $10$ colors in total, and adjacent balls (in neighboring boxes) have different colors. Each ball has an integer written on it. It is known that for every pair of colors, the sum of the integers on all balls of those two colors is equal (i.e., the sum of integers over balls of each color is the same for all $10$ colors). Find the minimum possible value of $n$.

*GCD-min inequality and equality cases for positive integers*

> Let $a, b$ be positive integers. Here $\gcd(x, y)$ denotes the greatest common divisor of $x$ and $y$, and $\min(x, y)$ denotes the smaller of $x$ and $y$.
> 
> (1) Show that the inequality
> $$\min\bigl(\gcd(a,\, b+1),\; \gcd(a+1,\, b)\bigr) \le \frac{\sqrt{4a+5}-1}{2}$$
> holds.
> 
> (2) Find all pairs of positive integers $(a, b)$ for which equality holds in the above inequality.

*Minimum orthogonal moves in king-move Hamiltonian path on n×n grid*

> Let $n \ge 3$ be an integer. Consider an $n \times n$ grid of squares. The following game is played:
> 
> \begin{itemize}
> \item Choose one square and place a piece on it.
> \item At each step, from the square currently occupied by the piece, choose one square adjacent horizontally, vertically, or diagonally that has not yet been visited, and move the piece there. Repeat this operation until no such unvisited adjacent square exists.
> \end{itemize}
> 
> When all $n^2$ squares have been visited, find the minimum possible number of times the piece moved horizontally or vertically (i.e., non-diagonally) during the game.

**Metodo:** [[method_invarianti|Invarianti]], [[method_estremalita|Estremalità]], [[method_casework|Casework]]

*Mosse ortogonali minime nel percorso hamiltoniano a mosse di re su griglia n×n*

> Sia $n \ge 3$ un numero intero. Si consideri una griglia di $n \times n$ caselle quadrate. Si gioca il seguente gioco:
>
> \begin{itemize}
> \item Scegli un quadrato e colloca una pedina su di esso. \item Ad ogni passo, partendo dal quadrato attualmente occupato dalla pedina, scegli un quadrato adiacente in orizzontale, in verticale o in diagonale che non sia stato ancora visitato, e sposta la pedina lì. Ripeti questa operazione finché non esiste alcun quadrato adiacente non visitato di questo tipo.
> \end{itemize}
>
> Quando tutti i quadrati $n^2$ sono stati visitati, determina il numero minimo possibile di volte in cui il pezzo si è mosso orizzontalmente o verticalmente (ossia in modo non diagonale) durante la partita.

*Angle equality in configuration with circumcircles and midpoints*

> Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB \neq AC$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of side $BC$, and let $N$ be the midpoint of the arc $BC$ (the arc containing $A$) of the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$. Let $H$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $N$ to line $AC$. Let $K$ be the intersection point, other than $C$, of the circumcircle of triangle $AMC$ and line $CN$. Prove that $\angle AKH = \angle CAM$. (Here $XY$ denotes the length of segment $XY$.)
