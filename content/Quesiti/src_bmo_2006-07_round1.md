*Quadrilateral with trisection points on two sides; prove equal third-areas*

> In the quadrilateral $ABCD$, points $M$ and $N$ are on side $AB$ such that $AM = MN = NB$, and points $P$ and $Q$ are on side $CD$ such that $CP = PQ = QD$. Prove that
> $$\text{Area of } AMCP = \text{Area of } MNQP = \tfrac{1}{3} \text{ Area of } ABCD.$$

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]]
**Metodo:** [[method_coordinate|Coordinate]], [[method_simmetria|Simmetria]]

*Find the unique 9-digit pandigital number with n-divisibility property*

> The number $3816547290$ is the only positive integer with ten digits (using each digit exactly once) such that the first $n$ digits form a number divisible by $n$ for each $n = 1, 2, \ldots, 10$.
> 
> Find the only 9-digit positive integer (using each of the digits $1$ to $9$ exactly once) such that the first $n$ digits form a number divisible by $n$ for each $n = 1, 2, \ldots, 9$.

*Two tangent circles with common tangent; diameter AP; tangent PQ; prove AP = PQ*

> Two touching circles $S$ and $T$ have a common tangent which meets $S$ at $A$ and $T$ at $B$. Let $AP$ be a diameter of $S$ and let the tangent from $P$ to $T$ touch it at $Q$. Prove that $AP = PQ$.

**Metodo:** [[method_trigonometria|Trigonometria]], [[method_simmetria|Simmetria]]
