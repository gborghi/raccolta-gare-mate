*Find unique points on rays so three triangles share perimeter 2s*

> From a point $O$ in 3-D space, three rays $OA$, $OB$, $OC$ emerge, the angles $BOC$, $COA$, $AOB$ being $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$ respectively. $0 < \alpha, \beta, \gamma < \pi$. Prove that, given $2s > 0$, there are unique points $X$, $Y$, $Z$ on $OA$, $OB$, $OC$ respectively such that the triangles $YOZ$, $ZOX$ and $XOY$ all have the same perimeter $2s$, and express $OX$ in terms of $s$ and $\sin\alpha$, $\sin\beta$, $\sin\gamma$.

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_solida|Geometria solida]], [[topic_trigonometria|Trigonometria]]
**Metodo:** [[method_simmetria|Simmetria]], [[method_trigonometria|Trigonometria]]

*Sum inequality for set of distinct positive odd integers with distinct differences*

> $S$ is a set of distinct positive odd integers $\{a_i\}$, $i = 1$ to $n$. No two differences $|a_i - a_j|$ are equal, $1 \le i < j \le n$. Prove that $$\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i \ge \frac{1}{3}n(n^2+2).$$

*Partition function satisfies convexity inequality p(n+1)-2p(n)+p(n-1)>=0*

> For $m$ a positive integer denote by $p(m)$ the number of ways of expressing $m$ as the sum of one or more positive integers. Thus $p(4) = 5$, because there are 5 different ways, namely $1+1+1+1$, $1+1+2$, $1+3$, $2+2$, $4$. Prove that for $n > 1$, $$p(n+1) - 2p(n) + p(n-1) \ge 0.$$

*La funzione di partizione soddisfa l'ineguaglianza di convessità p(n+1)-2p(n)+p(n-1)>=0*

> Per $m$ intero positivo, sia $p(m)$ il numero di modi di esprimere $m$ come somma di uno o più interi positivi. Così $p(4) = 5$, perché ci sono 5 modi diversi, vale a dire $1+1+1+1$, $1+1+2$, $1+3$, $2+2$, $4$. Dimostrare che per $n > 1$,
> $$p(n+1) - 2p(n) + p(n-1) \ge 0.$$
