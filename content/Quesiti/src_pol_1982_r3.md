*Cover finite plane point set by open squares with bounded points-per-area ratio*

> On a plane is given a finite set of points. Prove that the points can be covered by open squares $Q_1, Q_2, \ldots, Q_n$ such that
> $$1 \le \frac{N_j}{S_j} \le 4 \quad \text{for } j = 1, \ldots, n,$$
> where $N_j$ is the number of points from the set inside square $Q_j$ and $S_j$ is the area of $Q_j$.

**Topic:** [[topic_combinatoria|Combinatoria]], [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]]
**Metodo:** [[method_estremalita|Estremalità]], [[method_casework|Casework]], [[method_induzione|Induction]]

**Area:** [[Combinatoria, Logica e Probabilita|Combinatorics, logic and probability]], [[Geometria|Geometry]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qBPh1W7EiylRWktVfDLoEOsdrnYFDxsy/view)

*Cyclic integer sequence with sign condition: prove a difference of sums is divisible by 4*

> Integers $x_0, x_1, \ldots, x_{n-1}, x_n = x_0, x_{n+1} = x_1$ satisfy the inequality $(-1)^k x_{k-1} x_{k+1} > 0$ for $k = 1, 2, \ldots, n$. Prove that the difference
> $$\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} x_k - \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} |x_k|$$
> is divisible by $4$.
