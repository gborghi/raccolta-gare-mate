*Foot of perpendiculars from point to triangle sides ratio*

> $P$ is a point inside a given triangle $ABC$, $D$, $E$, $F$ are the feet of the perpendiculars from $P$ to the lines $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ respectively. Find all $P$ for which
> $$\frac{BC}{PD} + \frac{CA}{PE} + \frac{AB}{PF}$$
> is least.

*Rapporto tra i piedi delle perpendicolari da un punto ai lati di un triangolo*

*Smallest element subset mean function identity*

> Let $1 \le r \le n$ and consider all subsets of $r$ elements of the set $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$. Each of these subsets has a smallest member. Let $F(n, r)$ denote the arithmetic mean of these smallest numbers. Prove that
> $$F(n, r) = \frac{n+1}{r+1}.$$

*Identità della funzione media del sottoinsieme con elemento minimo*

> Sia $1 \le r \le n$ e si considerino tutti i sottoinsiemi di $r$ elementi dell'insieme $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$. Ogni uno di questi sottoinsiemi ha un elemento minimo. Sia $F(n, r)$ la media aritmetica di questi elementi minimi. Si dimostri che
> $$F(n, r) = \frac{n+1}{r+1}.$$

*Largest number in set divides LCM of remaining n-1*

> (a) For which values of $n > 2$ is there a set of $n$ consecutive positive integers such that the largest number in the set is a divisor of the least common multiple of the remaining $n - 1$ numbers?
> 
> (b) For which values of $n > 2$ is there exactly one such set having the stated property?
