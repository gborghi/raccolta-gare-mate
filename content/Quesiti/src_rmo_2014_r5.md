*Find positive integer values a and b given AP and GP conditions on roots*

> The roots of the equation
> $$x^3 - 3ax^2 + bx + 18c = 0$$
> form a non-constant arithmetic progression and the roots of the equation
> $$x^3 + bx^2 + x - c^2 = 0$$
> form a non-constant geometric progression. Given that $a$, $b$, $c$ are real numbers, find all positive integral values $a$ and $b$.

*Angle bisectors from perpendicular bisectors in acute triangle; prove BO perp to DE*

> Let $ABC$ be an acute-angled triangle in which $\angle ABC$ is the largest angle. Let $O$ be its circumcentre. The perpendicular bisectors of $BC$ and $AC$ meet $AB$ and $BC$ at $X$ and $Y$ respectively. The internal bisectors of $\angle AXB$ and $\angle BYC$ meet $AC$ at $D$ and $E$ respectively. Prove that $BO$ is perpendicular to $DE$.

Le bisettrici interne di $\angle AXB$ e $\angle BYC$ incontrano $AC$ rispettivamente in $D$ e $E$. Dimostrare che $BO$ è perpendicolare a $DE$.

*Count distinct paths from (0,0) to (8,8) with exactly three rightward steps*

> A person moves in the $x$-$y$ plane moving along points with integer co-ordinates $x$ and $y$ only. When she is at point $(x, y)$, she takes a step based on the following rules:
> (a) if $x + y$ is even she moves to either $(x+1, y)$ or $(x-1, y+1)$;
> (b) if $x + y$ is odd she moves to either $(x+1, y)$ or $(x+1, y+1)$.
> How many distinct paths can she take to go from $(0, 0)$ to $(8, 8)$ given that she took exactly three steps to the right $\{(x, y) \to (x+1, y)\}$?

*Conta i percorsi distinti da (0,0) a (8,8) con esattamente tre passi verso destra*

> Una persona si muove nel piano $x$-$y$, spostandosi soltanto lungo punti a coordinate intere $x$ e $y$. Quando si trova nel punto $(x, y)$, compie un passo secondo le seguenti regole:
> (a) se $x + y$ è pari, si sposta in $(x+1, y)$ oppure in $(x-1, y+1)$;
> (b) se $x + y$ è dispari, si sposta in $(x+1, y)$ oppure in $(x+1, y+1)$.
> Quanti percorsi distinti può seguire per andare da $(0, 0)$ a $(8, 8)$, sapendo che ha compiuto esattamente tre passi verso destra $\{(x, y) \to (x+1, y)\}$?
