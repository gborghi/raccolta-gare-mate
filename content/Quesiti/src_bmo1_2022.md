*Three even numbers under 400 as sums of consecutive odd numbers in 6 ways*

> Find three even numbers less than 400, each of which can be expressed as a sum of consecutive positive odd numbers in at least six different ways.
> 
> (Two expressions are considered to be different if they contain different numbers. The order of the numbers forming a sum is irrelevant.)

*Count pile arrangements of gold pieces of weight powers of 2 totalling 2021 grams*

> For each integer $0 \le n \le 11$, Eliza has exactly three identical pieces of gold that weigh $2^n$ grams. In how many different ways can she form a pile of gold weighing 2021 grams?
> 
> (Two piles are different if they contain different numbers of gold pieces of some weight. The arrangement of the pieces in the piles is irrelevant.)

*Two intersecting circles with point C on minor arc; prove triangle DO1E equilateral*

> Two circles $\Gamma_1$ and $\Gamma_2$ have centres $O_1$ and $O_2$ respectively. They pass through each other's centres and intersect at $A$ and $B$. The point $C$ lies on the minor arc $BO_2$ of $\Gamma_1$. The points $D$ and $E$ lie on the line $O_2C$ such that $\angle AO_1D = \angle DO_1C$ and $\angle CO_1E = \angle EO_1B$. Prove that triangle $DO_1E$ is equilateral.
> 
> (A minor arc of a circle is the shorter of the two arcs with given endpoints.)

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]]
**Metodo:** [[method_simmetria|Simmetria]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_ragionamento_geometrico|Ragionamento geometrico]], [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_lettura_attenta|Lettura attenta]]
**Area:** [[Geometria]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ldnNBiWXMyvCmwHuX6A7qXhF4AqgZKYU/view)


<span class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></span>


*Due cerchi che si intersecano con il punto C su un arco minore; prova triangolo DO1E equilaterale*

> Due cerchi $\Gamma_1$ e $\Gamma_2$ hanno rispettivamente i centri $O_1$ e $O_2$. Passano attraverso i centri dell'altro e si incrociano a $A$ e $B$. Il punto $C$ si trova sull'arco minore $BO_2$ di $\Gamma_1$. I punti $D$ e $E$ si trovano sulla linea $O_2C$ in modo tale che $\angle AO_1D = \angle DO_1C$ e $\angle CO_1E = \angle EO_1B$. Prova che il triangolo $DO_1E$ è equilaterale.
> 
> (Un arco minore di un cerchio è il più breve dei due archi con determinati punti terminali.)

*Count lists of integers with 71 terms, first term 1, recursive rule, sum 999999*

> Marvin has been tasked with writing down every list of integers with the following properties:
> 
> (i) The list contains 71 terms.
> 
> (ii) The first term is 1.
> 
> (iii) Every term after the first is equal to either the previous term, or the sum of all previous terms.
> 
> When Marvin is finished, how many of the lists will have a sum equal to 999,999?
