*Find which n in 3..12 admit a happy circle arrangement*

> We say that a number $n \ge 3$ is $happy$ if it is possible to arrange the numbers $1$ to $n$ in a circle such that the following two conditions hold:
> (a) If $x$ and $y$ are neighbours in the circle, then $x$ divides $y$ or $y$ divides $x$;
> (b) If different integers $u$ and $v$ are in the circle but are not neighbours, then neither divides the other.
> 
> Find all $n$ in the range $3 \le n \le 12$ which are happy.

*Trova quale n in 3.12 ammette una disposizione di cerchio felice*

> Diciamo che un numero $n \ge 3$ è $happy$ se è possibile organizzare i numeri $1$ a $n$ in un cerchio in modo tale che si applichino le seguenti due condizioni: (a) Se $x$ e $y$ sono vicini nel cerchio, allora $x$ divide $y$ o $y$ divide $x$; (b) Se diversi interi $u$ e $v$ sono nel cerchio ma non sono vicini, allora nessuno divide l'altro.
> 
> Trova tutte le $n$ nella gamma $3 \le n \le 12$ che sono soddisfatte.

*Cards in a circle, neighbour sums divisible by 3*

> The numbers $1, 2, \dots, n$ are written on $n$ cards, one number on each card. The cards are placed in a circle. (i) If the cards are placed in a particular order, the sum of the numbers on each pair of neighbouring cards is divisible by $3$. (ii) Determine all values of $n$ for which the cards can be placed in a circle so that the sum of the numbers on each pair of neighbouring cards is divisible by $3$.

*Game replacing n by |a-b| starting at 10^6*

> Rhian and Jack are playing a game in which initially the number $10^6$ is written on a blackboard. If the number $n$ on the blackboard can be written as $a + b$ for two different positive integers $a$ and $b$, a move consists of choosing such $a$ and $b$ and replacing $n$ with $|a - b|$. Rhian starts, then the players make moves alternately. A player who is unable to move loses.
> 
> Determine, with proof, who wins the game.

*Acute triangle, P on median AM with AB=CP, prove angle CPB=90*

> In the acute-angled triangle $ABC$ we have $AB < AC < BC$. The midpoint of $BC$ is $M$. There is a point $P$ on the line segment $AM$ such that $AB = CP$, and $\angle PAB = \angle BCP$.
> 
> Prove that $\angle CPB = 90^\circ$.

*Smallest n not dividing p; prove divisibility by p*

> Let $p$ be a prime number, and let $n$ be the smallest positive integer, strictly greater than $1$, which is not a divisor of $p - 1$. Prove that $(n+1)^k - 1$ and $(n+1)^k - n$ are divisible by $p$ for some positive integer $k$.

*4x4x4 cube of 3-flavour sugar cubes; find 12 same-flavour in 9 equal-distance pairs*

> Bjork has $64$ sugar cubes, each of size $1 \times 1 \times 1$. Each sugar cube is either white or demerara or muscovado in flavour. She piles the sugar cubes into a $4 \times 4 \times 4$ cube. Prove that there must be $12$ sugar cubes of the same flavour amongst those cubes whose centres lie on a $1 \times 1 \times 1$ axis-aligned sublattice, which can be split into $9$ disjoint pairs so that the cubes of each pair are the same colour.

*4x4x4 cubo di zuccheri a 3 sapori; trovare 12 sapori identici in 9 coppie di distanza uguale*

> Bjork ha cubetti di zucchero $64$, ciascuno di dimensioni $1 \times 1 \times 1$. Ogni cubo di zucchero ha un sapore bianco, demeraro o muscovado. Ha accumulato i cubetti di zucchero in un cubo $4 \times 4 \times 4$. Dovere che tra i cubetti i cui centri si trovano su una sottolattice allineata all'asse $1 \times 1 \times 1$ devono esistere cubetti di zucchero $12$ dello stesso sapore, che possono essere suddivisi in coppie disgiunte $9$ in modo che i cubetti di ciascuna coppia siano dello stesso colore.
