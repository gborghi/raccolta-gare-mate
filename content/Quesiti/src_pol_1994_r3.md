*Mappings on subsets respecting intersection-min, limit of n-th root*

> Given a fixed integer $c \ge 1$, let $a(n)$ be the number of mappings $w$ from the subsets of $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$ to the integers $1, 2, \ldots, c$ such that
> $$w(A \cap B) = \min\{w(A), w(B)\} \quad \text{for any two subsets } A, B \text{ of } \{1, \ldots, n\}.$$
> Compute $\lim_{n \to \infty} \sqrt[n]{a(n)}$.

*Three vessels, coprime capacities m and n, pour to get any k liters*

> We are given three vessels without the scale: two of them of capacities $m$ and $n$ liters are empty, and the third one of capacity $m + n$ liters is full of water, where $m$ and $n$ are coprime positive integers. Prove that for any $k = 1, 2, \ldots, m + n - 1$, pouring out from one vessel into another, we can obtain exactly $k$ liters of water in the third vessel.

*Tre recipienti, con capacità m e n coprime, versano per ottenere k litri qualsiasi*

*n reals, zero sum, unit sum of squares, four satisfy double inequality with sum of cubes*

> Let $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n$ ($n \ge 4$) be different real numbers which satisfy the conditions $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i = 0$ and $\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i^2 = 1$. Show that there exist four of these numbers, say $a, b, c, d$, such that
> $$a + b + c + nabc \le \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i^3 \le a + b + d + nabd.$$
