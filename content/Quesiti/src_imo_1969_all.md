*Common zeros of weighted cosine sum differ by multiple of pi*

> Let $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ be real constants, $x$ be a real variable, and
> $$f(x) = \cos(a_1 + x) + \frac{1}{2}\cos(a_2 + x) + \frac{1}{4}\cos(a_3 + x) + \cdots + \frac{1}{2^{n-1}}\cos(a_n + x).$$
> Given that $f(x_1) = f(x_2) = 0$, prove that $x_2 - x_1$ is a rational multiple of $\pi$.

**Topic:** [[topic_trigonometria|Trigonometria]]
**Metodo:** [[method_trigonometria|Tecniche trigonometriche]]
**Area:** [[Geometria]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF p.1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12FvNr0jk5CMfk7r3Xncci7U9Z2FtRov9/view)

*Prove quadratic-form inequality with equality conditions*

> Prove that for all real numbers $x_1, x_2, y_1, y_2, z_1, z_2$ with $x_1 > 0$, $x_2 > 0$, $x_1 y_1 - z_1^2 > 0$, $x_2 y_2 - z_2^2 > 0$, the following inequality is satisfied:
> $$\frac{8}{(x_1 + x_2)(y_1 + y_2) - (z_1 + z_2)^2} \leq \frac{1}{x_1 y_1 - z_1^2} + \frac{1}{x_2 y_2 - z_2^2}.$$

**Topic:** [[topic_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]]

**Fonte:** [apri PDF p.1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12FvNr0jk5CMfk7r3Xncci7U9Z2FtRov9/view)
