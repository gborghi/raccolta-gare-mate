*Minimum number of distinct values among 14 subset sums*

> Let $a, b, c, d$ be pairwise distinct positive real numbers. Among the following real numbers
> $$a,\quad b,\quad c,\quad d,\quad a+b,\quad a+c,\quad a+d,\quad b+c,\quad b+d,\quad c+d,$$
> $$a+b+c,\quad a+b+d,\quad a+c+d,\quad b+c+d,\quad a+b+c+d$$
> the number of distinct values that appear was exactly $k$. Find the minimum possible value of $k$.

*Four points concyclic from orthocenter angle bisector and circumcircle*

> Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle satisfying $AB < AC$ and $\angle BAC = 60^\circ$, and let $H$ be its orthocenter. Let $P$ be the intersection of the bisector of $\angle BHC$ with side $BC$. Among the intersection points of the perpendicular bisector of side $BC$ with the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$, let $M$ be the one lying on the same side of line $BC$ as $A$, and let $N$ be the one lying on the opposite side. Show that the four points $H$, $M$, $N$, $P$ lie on a single circle. Here, $XY$ denotes the length of the segment $XY$.

*Minimum stones left in box 1 after stone-moving operations*

> There are $2025$ boxes numbered $1, 2, \ldots, 2025$, and for each integer $i$ with $1 \le i \le 2025$, box $i$ initially contains $2^{i-1}$ stones. The following operation is repeated.
> 
> Choose an integer $n \ge 2$ and integers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ satisfying $1 \le a_1 < a_2 < \cdots < a_n \le 2025$ such that for every integer $i$ with $2 \le i \le n$, box $a_i$ contains at least one stone. Put $1$ stone into box $a_1$, and for each integer $i$ with $2 \le i \le n$, remove one stone from box $a_i$.
> 
> When the state is reached in which no box other than box $1$ contains any stones, the operation is terminated. Find the minimum possible value of the number of stones in box $1$ when the operation has terminated.

*Minimum di pietre lasciate nella casella 1 dopo le operazioni di spostamento delle pietre*

> Ci sono caselle $2025$ numerate $1, 2, \ldots, 2025$ e per ogni numero intero $i$ con $1 \le i \le 2025$, la casella $i$ contiene inizialmente le pietre $2^{i-1}$. Si ripete la seguente operazione.
> 
> Selezionare un intero $n \ge 2$ e un intero $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ che soddisfi $1 \le a_1 < a_2 < \cdots < a_n \le 2025$ in modo tale che per ogni intero $i$ con $2 \le i \le n$, la casella $a_i$ contenga almeno una pietra. Mettere la pietra $1$ nella casella $a_1$ e togliere una pietra dalla casella $a_i$ per ogni numero intero $i$ con $2 \le i \le n$.
> 
> Quando si raggiunge lo stato in cui nessuna casella diversa dalla casella $1$ contiene pietre, l'operazione viene interrotta. Trova il valore minimo possibile del numero di pietre nella casella $1$ quando l'operazione è terminata.

*Functional equation involving the floor function*

> Find all functions $f$ defined on the reals and taking real values such that for all real numbers $x, y$,
> $$f(x)\,f\big(f(x) + y\big) = f(x)^2 + [x][y]$$
> holds. Here, for a real number $r$, $[r]$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $r$; for example, $[3.14] = 3$, $[5] = 5$, $[-2.71] = -3$.

*Equazione funzionale che coinvolge la funzione del pavimento*

> Trovare tutte le funzioni $f$ definite sui numeri reali e prendere valori reali in modo tale che per tutti i numeri reali $x, y$, $$f(x)\,f\big(f(x) + y\big) = f(x)^2 + [x][y]$$ sia valido. Qui, per un numero reale $r$, $[r]$ indica il numero intero più grande inferiore o uguale a $r$; ad esempio $[3.14] = 3$, $[5] = 5$, $[-2.71] = -3$.
