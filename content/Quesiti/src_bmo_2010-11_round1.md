*Cube side a with square hole; volume equals surface area numerically*

> Let $a$ be an integer greater than $1$. A solid cube of side $a$ has a square hole of side $s$ drilled directly through from one face to the opposite face (so the drill removes a cuboid). The volume of the remaining solid is numerically equal to the total surface area of the remaining solid. Determine all possible values of $a$.

*Lato a del cubo con foro quadrato; il volume è numericamente uguale all'area superficiale*

> Sia $a$ un numero intero maggiore di $1$. Un cubo solido di lato $a$ presenta un foro quadrato di lato $s$, praticato direttamente da una faccia all'opposta (cosicché la punta foratrice asporta un parallelepipedo rettangolo). Il volume del solido rimanente è numericamente uguale all'area totale della superficie del solido rimanente. Determinare tutti i valori possibili di $a$.

*8x8 board, red/white/blue coins; count arrangements with odd red per row and column*

> Isaac has a large supply of coins, and places one on each square of an $8 \times 8$ chessboard. Each coin is either red, white or blue. A colouring is called an arrangement if, in each row and each column, there are an odd number of red coins. Find the number of such arrangements.

*Integers a,b,c with a^2+b^2-c^2=ab-bc-ca; prove triangle with 60 degree angle*

> Let $a$ and $b$ be positive integers and let $c$ be a positive integer less than $a + b$. Suppose that $a^2 + b^2 - c^2 = ab - bc - ca$. Show that $a$, $b$, $c$ are the sides of a triangle that contains an angle of $60^\circ$.
