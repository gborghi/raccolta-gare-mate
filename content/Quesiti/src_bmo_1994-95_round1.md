*Find max and min of cyclic expression over unit cube*

> (a) Find the maximum value of the expression $x^2 y + y^2 z + z^2 x$ when $0 \le x \le 1$, $0 \le y \le 1$, $0 \le z \le 1$.
> 
> (b) Find the minimum value of the expression $x^2 y + y^2 z + z^2 x$ when $0 \le x \le 1$, $0 \le y \le 1$, $0 \le z \le 1$.

*Right-angled triangle; angle bisectors; feet of perpendiculars to hypotenuse; find MN*

> $ABC$ is a triangle, right-angled at $C$. The internal bisectors of angles $BAC$ and $ABC$ meet $BC$ and $CA$ at $P$ and $Q$ respectively. The points $M$ and $N$ are the feet of the perpendiculars from $P$ and $Q$ to $AB$. Find $MN$ in terms of the lengths of the sides of the triangle.

*Triangolo rettangolo; bisetti d'angolo; piedi perpendicolari all'ipotenusa; trovare MN*

*Seven dwarfs queue by height rule; count valid queues with and without Snow White*

> The seven dwarfs decide to queue. They insist on queuing so that, in each group of consecutive dwarfs in the queue, the tallest dwarf in that group is at the front. How many such queues are possible?
> 
> What if Snow White always comes along too?
