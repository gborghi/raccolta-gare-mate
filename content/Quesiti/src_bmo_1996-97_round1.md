*Reverse digits of N equals 4N+3; find all four-digit N*

> $N$ is a four-digit integer, not ending in zero, and $B(N)$ is the four-digit integer obtained by reversing the digits of $N$. For example, $B(3275) = 5723$.
> 
> Determine all such integers $N$ for which $B(N) = 4N + 3$.

*Gli numeri inversi di N sono uguali a 4N+3; trovare tutti i N a quattro cifre *

> $N$ è un numero intero a quattro cifre, che non termina in zero, e $B(N)$ è il numero intero a quattro cifre ottenuto invertendo i numeri di $N$. Ad esempio, $B(3275) = 5723$.
> 
> Determinare tutti i numeri interi $N$ per i quali $B(N) = 4N + 3$.

*Sequence with a_{n+1} defined by digit sum; find a_{1997}*

> For positive integers $n \geq 1$, define the sequence $a_1, a_2, a_3, \ldots$ by
> $$a_1 = 1, \quad a_{n+1} = a_n + \frac{n+1}{\text{largest prime factor of }(n+1)}, \quad n \geq 1.$$
> Determine the value of $a_{1997}$.

*Quadrilateral with inscribed circle; prove PQRS area half of ABCD*

> Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral which has an inscribed circle (i.e., a circle touching all four sides). The midpoints of $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DA$ are $P$, $Q$, $R$, $S$ respectively. Prove that the area of the quadrilateral $PQRS$ is half the area of $ABCD$.

*Quadrilaterale con un cerchio inciso; dimostrare la superficie PQRS metà di ABCD*

*Positive reals x, y with x+y>=2; two inequality questions*

> Let $x$ and $y$ be positive real numbers.
> 
> (a) If $x + y \geq 2$, is it necessarily true that $\dfrac{1}{x} + \dfrac{1}{y} \geq 2$?
> 
> (b) If $x + y \geq 2$, is it necessarily true that $\dfrac{1}{x^2} + \dfrac{1}{y^2} \geq 2$?
