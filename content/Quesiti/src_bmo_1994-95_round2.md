*Prove angle equality iff another angle equality in triangle midpoint configuration*

> Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and $D$, $E$, $F$ be the midpoints of $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ respectively. Prove that $\angle DAC = \angle ABE$ if and only if $\angle AFC = \angle ADB$.

*Count pairings of 2n people; prove divisibility of multinomial*

> (a) Determine, with careful explanation, how many ways $2n$ people can be paired off to form $n$ teams of $2$.
> 
> (b) Prove that $(2mn)!$ is divisible by $((m!)^n (n!)^m)^2$ for all positive integers $m$, $n$.
