*5x5 grid of integers 1-25: choose five numbers, no two in the same line or column; find a set with largest element 23, and one whose largest element is smallest possible*

> On the board below are written all the integers from $1$ to $25$. Consider all the sets formed by five of these numbers, in such a way that there are no two numbers belonging to the same line or the same column.
> 
> $$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|}\hline 2 & 13 & 16 & 11 & 23 \\ \hline 15 & 1 & 9 & 7 & 10 \\ \hline 14 & 12 & 21 & 24 & 8 \\ \hline 3 & 25 & 22 & 18 & 4 \\ \hline 20 & 19 & 6 & 5 & 17 \\ \hline \end{array}$$
> 
> a) Present a set whose largest element is $23$.
> 
> b) Present a set whose largest element is the smallest possible.

*X is reflection of B across line r (perpendicular bisector setup); X' Y' Z' are reflections of vertices of triangle XYZ across the opposite sides; compute the area of triangle X'Y'Z'*

> In the drawing, the line $r$ is perpendicular to the segment $AB$ and passes through its midpoint $M$. We say that $X$ is the symmetric (reflection) of $B$ with respect to the line $r$ when $r$ is the perpendicular bisector of segment $BX$ (analogously for the symmetric of a point with respect to segment $PQ$).
> 
> Let the triangle $X'Y'Z'$ be such that $X'$ is the symmetric of $X$ with respect to the side $YZ$, $Y'$ is the symmetric of $Y$ with respect to the side $XZ$, and $Z'$ is the symmetric of $Z$ with respect to the side $XY$.
> 
> Compute the area of triangle $X'Y'Z'$.

*On an 8x8 board two players alternately place 1x1 pawns on squares; the player who cannot move loses; determine which player has a winning strategy independent of the opponent's play*

> Two players play on a chessboard ($8 \times 8$) made up of squares the size of the pieces. The two players play alternately, and on each play a player places a $1 \times 1$ pawn on an empty square of the board. The player who cannot place a pawn on the board loses. Can a player win independently of how the adversary plays — that is, does one of the players have a winning strategy?

Il giocatore che non riesce a mettere un pedone sulla tavola perde. Un giocatore può vincere indipendentemente dal modo in cui l'avversario gioca  cioè, uno dei giocatori ha una strategia vincente?
