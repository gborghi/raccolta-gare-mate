*Count and characterize 'robust' and 'super-robust' 8-digit numbers*

> An eight-digit number is called *robust* if it satisfies both of the following conditions:
> 
> (i) None of its digits is $0$.
> 
> (ii) The difference between any two consecutive digits is $4$ or $5$.
> 
> Answer the following questions:
> 
> (a) How many robust numbers are there?
> 
> (b) A robust number is called *super-robust* if all its digits are distinct. Compute the sum of all super-robust numbers.

*Prove inequality involving min and max of two positive integers*

> Let $a$, $b$, and $k$ be positive integers with $k > 1$ such that
> $$\min(a, b) + \text{mdc}(a, b) = k(a + b),$$
> where $\text{mdc}(a,b)$ denotes the greatest common divisor of $a$ and $b$.
> 
> Prove that $a + b \ge 4k$.

*Circumscribed circle intersection: show XD and AQ meet on circle*

> Let $ABC$ be a right triangle inscribed in a circle $\Gamma$ with center $O$. Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from vertex $A$. Let $E$ and $F$ be points on $\Gamma$ such that $AE = AD = AF$. Let $P$ and $Q$ be the intersection points of line $EF$ with sides $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. Let $X$ be the second intersection point of line $EF$ with the circumscribed circle of triangle $APQ$. Show that the lines $XD$ and $AQ$ meet on $\Gamma$.
