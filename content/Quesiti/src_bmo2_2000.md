*Common tangent and equal areas of triangles from two intersecting circles*

> Two intersecting circles $C_1$ and $C_2$ have a common tangent which touches $C_1$ at $P$ and $C_2$ at $Q$. The two circles intersect at $M$ and $N$, where $N$ is nearer to $PQ$ than $M$. Prove that the triangles $MNP$ and $MNQ$ have equal areas.

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]]
**Metodo:** [[method_simmetria|Simmetria]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_ragionamento_geometrico|Ragionamento geometrico]], [[skill_lettura_attenta|Lettura attenta]]
**Area:** [[Geometria]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BEx1MRf0HGALRwfMJ0zD4xZnI7CsxPCd/view)


<span class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></span>


*Aree tangenti comuni e uguali di triangoli da due cerchi intersezionati*

> Due cerchi incrociati $C_1$ e $C_2$ hanno una tangente comune che tocca $C_1$ a $P$ e $C_2$ a $Q$. I due cerchi si incrociano a $M$ e $N$, dove $N$ è più vicino a $PQ$ rispetto a $M$. Provare che i triangoli $MNP$ e $MNQ$ hanno aree uguali.

*Set of ten distinct elements with sum divisible by 6; replace ten with seven*

> (a) Find a set $A$ of ten distinct elements such that six distinct elements of $A$ have a sum which is divisible by 6. (b) Is it possible to find such a set when 'ten' is replaced by 'seven'?
