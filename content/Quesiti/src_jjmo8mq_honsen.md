*Largest k so truncating a perfect square keeps squares*

> Find the largest nonnegative integer $k$ for which there exists a positive integer $n$ satisfying the following condition.
> 
> Condition: $n$ is a perfect square with at least $k+1$ digits, and for every positive integer $i$ with $i \le k$, the number obtained from $n$ by deleting its last $i$ digits is also a perfect square.

*Concyclicity of A, incenter I, and two circle points P, Q*

> Consider a triangle $ABC$. A circle $O$ passing through the points $B$ and $C$ meets the segments $AB$ and $AC$ (excluding their endpoints) at points $D$ and $E$ respectively, and $AD + AE = BC$ holds. Let $I$ be the incenter of triangle $ABC$, and let the lines $BI$ and $CI$ meet the circle $O$ at the points $P$ and $Q$ (other than $B$ and $C$) respectively. Show that $A$, $I$, $P$, $Q$ lie on a single circle.
> 
> Here, $XY$ denotes the length of the segment $XY$.

*Draw 2n white-black segments forming one tour with few crossings*

> Let $n$ be an integer with $n \ge 2$. On a circle there are $n$ white points and $n$ black points, $2n$ points in total. For these points, consider drawing $2n$ segments so as to satisfy the following conditions.
> 
> (1) Every segment has one white point and one black point as its endpoints.
> 
> (2) By following these segments in order, one can pass through every point exactly once and make one full loop (i.e. the segments form a single closed cycle through all the points).
> 
> Show that, for any arrangement of the points, the segments can be drawn so that the number of intersections between the segments is at most $n-1$. Here, an endpoint of a segment is not regarded as an intersection.

**Topic:** [[topic_combinatoria|Combinatoria]], [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]]
**Metodo:** [[method_estremalita|Estremalità]], [[method_casework|Casework]], [[method_induzione|Induzione]], [[method_grafi|Graph theory]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_conteggio_sistematico|Conteggio sistematico]], [[skill_ragionamento_geometrico|Ragionamento geometrico]], [[skill_casework_accurato|Careful case analysis]]
**Area:** [[Combinatoria, Logica e Probabilita|Combinatorics, logic and probability]], [[Geometria|Geometry]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1p2F4y-eHA_uMQrouWBYL9ACgMCFOr7Tx/view)


<div class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></div>


*Disegna 2n segmenti bianchi-neri che formano un unico percorso con pochi incroci*

> Sia $n$ un numero intero tale che $n \ge 2$. Su una circonferenza ci sono $n$ punti bianchi e $n$ punti neri, per un totale di $2n$ punti. Per questi punti, si consideri il tracciamento di $2n$ segmenti in modo da soddisfare le seguenti condizioni.
>
> (1) Ogni segmento ha come estremi un punto bianco e un punto nero.
>
> (2) Seguendo questi segmenti in ordine, si può passare per ogni punto esattamente una volta e compiere un giro completo (cioè i segmenti formano un unico ciclo chiuso che passa per tutti i punti).
>
> Dimostrare che, per qualsiasi disposizione dei punti, è possibile tracciare i segmenti in modo che il numero di intersezioni tra i segmenti sia al più $n-1$. Qui, un estremo di un segmento non è considerato un'intersezione.
