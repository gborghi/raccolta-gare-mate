*Two intersecting circles, line CF meets circles at P Q, midpoints of arcs, prove concyclic*

> As shown below, two circles $\Gamma_1$, $\Gamma_2$ intersect at points $A$, $B$. One line passing through $B$ intersects $\Gamma_1$, $\Gamma_2$ at points $C$, $D$; another line passing through $B$ intersects $\Gamma_1$, $\Gamma_2$ at points $E$, $F$; and line $CF$ intersects $\Gamma_1$, $\Gamma_2$ at points $P$, $Q$, respectively. Let $M$, $N$ be the middle points of arc $\widehat{PB}$ (on $\Gamma_1$) and arc $\widehat{QB}$ (on $\Gamma_2$), respectively. Draw lines $CM$ and $FN$. Prove that $C$, $F$, $M$, $N$ are concyclic. (Posed by Xiong Bin)

*Sequence with gcd-based recursion, prove it is prime infinitely often*

> Given an integer $k \ge 3$ and a sequence $\{a_n\}$ that satisfies $a_1 = 2k$ and for each $n > k$,
> $$a_{n+1} = \begin{cases} a_n + 1, & \text{if } a_n \text{ and } n \text{ are coprime,} \\ 2a_n, & \text{otherwise,} \end{cases}$$
> prove that $a_n$ is a prime for infinitely many $n$. (Posed by Zhu Huawei)

*Card piles at n+1 positions, two operations, prove n^2+3n+1 cards suffice*

> We operate on piles of cards placed at $n+1$ positions $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n$ ($n \ge 3$) and $O$. In one operation, we can do either of the following:
> (1) If there are at least three cards at some position $A_i$, we may take three cards from $A_i$ and place one card each at $A_{i-1}$, $A_i$, and $O$ (indices mod $n$, so $A_{n+1} = A_1$);
> (2) If there are at least $n$ cards at $O$, we may take $n$ cards from $O$ and place one card at each of $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n$.
> Prove that if the total number of cards is at least $n^2 + 3n + 1$, we can perform some sequence of operations such that there are at least $n+1$ cards at each of the positions $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n$. (Posed by Qu Zhenhua)

*Mucchi di carte nelle posizioni n+1, due operazioni, dimostrare che n^2+3n+1 carte bastano*

> Operiamo su pile di carte collocate nelle posizioni $n+1$ $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n$ ($n \ge 3$) e $O$. In un'operazione, possiamo fare una delle due cose seguenti: (1) Se in qualche posizione $A_i$ ci sono almeno tre carte, possiamo prelevare tre carte da $A_i$ e collocare una carta ciascuna in $A_{i-1}$, $A_i$ e $O$ (indici modulo $n$, quindi $A_{n+1} = A_1$); (2) Se in $O$ ci sono almeno $n$ carte, possiamo prelevare $n$ carte da $O$ e collocare una carta in ciascuna delle posizioni $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n$. Dimostrare che, se il numero totale di carte è almeno $n^2 + 3n + 1$, possiamo eseguire una qualche sequenza di operazioni tale che in ciascuna delle posizioni $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n$ ci siano almeno $n+1$ carte. (Proposto da Qu Zhenhua)

*Six pairwise distinct positive integers with power sum condition, prove ratio is integer*

> Let $a_1$, $a_2$, $a_3$, $b_1$, $b_2$, $b_3$ be pairwise distinct positive integers such that
> $$(n-1)!\, n!\, (n+1)!\, (2n-1)! \mid \prod_{1 \le i < j \le 3}(a_i - a_j) \cdot \prod_{1 \le i < j \le 3}(b_i - b_j)$$
> holds for some positive integer $n$. Prove that there exists a positive integer $r$ such that $a_1^r + a_2^r + a_3^r = b_1^r + b_2^r + b_3^r$. (Posed by Chen Yonggao)

*Sei interi positivi tutti distinti a coppie con condizione sulla somma delle potenze: dimostrare che il rapporto è un intero*

> Siano $a_1$, $a_2$, $a_3$, $b_1$, $b_2$, $b_3$ interi positivi due a due distinti tali che
> $$(n-1)!\, n!\, (n+1)!\, (2n-1)! \mid \prod_{1 \le i < j \le 3}(a_i - a_j) \cdot \prod_{1 \le i < j \le 3}(b_i - b_j)$$
> vale per qualche intero positivo $n$. Dimostrare che esiste un intero positivo $r$ tale che $a_1^r + a_2^r + a_3^r = b_1^r + b_2^r + b_3^r$. (Proposto da Chen Yonggao)
