*Four sequences satisfy coupled recurrences; given periodicity for some k, prove the second terms vanish.*

> Four sequences of real numbers $(a_n)$, $(b_n)$, $(c_n)$, $(d_n)$ satisfy for all $n$,
> $$a_{n+1}=a_n+b_n,\quad b_{n+1}=b_n+c_n,$$
> $$c_{n+1}=c_n+d_n,\quad d_{n+1}=d_n+a_n.$$
> Prove that if $a_{k+m}=a_m$, $b_{k+m}=b_m$, $c_{k+m}=c_m$, $d_{k+m}=d_m$ for some $k\ge 1$, $n\ge 1$, then $a_2=b_2=c_2=d_2=0$.

> Quattro successioni di numeri reali $(a_n)$, $(b_n)$, $(c_n)$, $(d_n)$ soddisfano, per ogni $n$,
> $$a_{n+1}=a_n+b_n,\quad b_{n+1}=b_n+c_n,$$
> $$c_{n+1}=c_n+d_n,\quad d_{n+1}=d_n+a_n.$$
> Dimostrare che, se $a_{k+m}=a_m$, $b_{k+m}=b_m$, $c_{k+m}=c_m$, $d_{k+m}=d_m$ per alcuni $k\ge 1$, $n\ge 1$, allora $a_2=b_2=c_2=d_2=0$.

*Optimize the number of net throws a trawler makes fishing illegally, given a per-throw probability of being caught and forfeiting all gains, to maximize expected profit.*

> A trawler is about to fish in territorial waters of a neighboring country, for what he has no licence. Whenever he throws the net, the coast-guard may stop him with the probability $1/k$, where $k$ is a fixed positive integer. Each throw brings him a fish landing of a fixed weight. However, if the coast-guard stops him, they will confiscate his entire fish landing and demand him to leave the country. The trawler plans to throw the net $n$ times before he returns to territorial waters in his country. Find $n$ for which his expected profit is maximal.

*An increasing function from N to R that is additive on products of naturals must be a logarithm.*

> An increasing function $f:\mathbb{N}\to\mathbb{R}$ satisfies
> $$f(kl)=f(k)+f(l)\quad\text{for all }k,l\in\mathbb{N}.$$
> Show that there is a real number $p>1$ such that $f(n)=\log_p n$ for all $n$.
