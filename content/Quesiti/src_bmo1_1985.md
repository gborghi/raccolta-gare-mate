*Two circles touching a line at a point, angle bisector ratio*

> Two circles $S_1$ and $S_2$ each touch a straight line $p$ at the same point $P$. All points of $S_2$, except $P$, are in the interior of $S_1$. A straight line $q$ (i) is perpendicular to $p$; (ii) touches $S_2$ at $R$; (iii) cuts $p$ at $L$; and (iv) cuts $S_1$ at $N$ and $M$, where $N$ is between $L$ and $M$.
> 
> (a) Prove that $q$ bisects angle $RPM$.
> 
> (b) If $MP$ bisects angle $RPL$, find, with proof, the ratio of the areas of $S_1$ and $S_2$.

*Due cerchi che toccano una retta nello stesso punto, rapporto di bisettrice di angolo*

Una retta $q$ (i) è perpendicolare a $p$; (ii) tocca $S_2$ in $R$; (iii) incontra $p$ in $L$; e (iv) incontra $S_1$ in $N$ e $M$, dove $N$ è tra $L$ e $M$.
> 
> (a) Dimostrare che $q$ biseca l'angolo $RPM$.
> 
> (b) Se $MP$ biseca l'angolo $RPL$, trovare, con dimostrazione, il rapporto delle aree di $S_1$ e $S_2$.

*Prove identity involving binomial coefficients product*

> $n$ and $m$ are non-negative integers. Prove that
> $$2 \binom{n+1}{1} + 3 \binom{n+2}{2} + \cdots + (r+1)\binom{n+r}{r} + \cdots + (m+1)\binom{n+m}{m} = \binom{n+m+2}{m},$$
> where $\binom{p}{r}$ is the binomial coefficient $p(p-1)(p-2)\cdots(p-r+1)/r!$.

*Sequence defined by recurrence; prove eventual periodicity*

> The sequence $f_n$ is defined by $f_0 = 1$, $f_1 = a$, where $a$ is a positive integer, and for all $n > 1$,
> $$f_n = 2f_{n-1} - f_{n-2} + 2.$$
> Prove that for each $k \geq 0$ there exists $h$ such that $f_h f_{h+1} = f_k$.
