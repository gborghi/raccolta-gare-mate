*a_n = last digit of digit-sum of 2005 written n times in a row; find n with a_n=0 and compute a sum*

> For every strictly positive integer $n$, define $a_n$ as the last digit of the sum of the digits of the number $2005\,2005\ldots 2005$ (one writes "$2005$" $n$ times in a row). For example, $a_1 = 7$ and $a_2 = 4$.

*Three-player goalkeeper game: count total phases and find who scored the 6th goal*

> Football with three people is played in successive phases: one player is the goalkeeper while the other two, called "field players", try to score a goal. As soon as a player scores, the phase ends and that player becomes the goalkeeper for the next phase. Amandine, Bobby and Charles play this game. When the game is over, they recall that Amandine was a field player $12$ times, Bobby $21$ times, and Charles $8$ times.
> a) How many phases were there in total?
> b) Who scored the sixth goal?

Gioco da portiere per tre giocatori: contare le fasi totali e trovare chi ha segnato il sesto gol

Appena un giocatore fa un gol, la fase termina e quel giocatore diventa il portiere per la fase successiva. Amandine, Bobby e Charles giocano a questo gioco. Quando la partita è finita, ricordano che Amandine era un giocatore di campo $12$ volte, Bobby $21$ volte, e Charles $8$ volte. a) Quante fasi ci sono state in totale? b) Chi ha segnato il sesto gol?

*n^2 with last two digits removed is again a perfect square*

> a) Find a strictly positive integer $n$ such that, if one writes $n^2$ and removes its last two digits, the resulting number is still the square of an integer.
> b) Find all strictly positive integers $n$ that are not multiples of $10$ such that, writing $n^2$ and deleting its last two digits, one again obtains the square of an integer (one considers that a number with zero digits is equal to zero).

*Rectangle ABCD, E = foot of perp from B to AC, circle through A and E centred on (AD), F on [CD]; prove BF bisects angle AFC*

Let $F$ be the point of intersection of $\Gamma$ and $[CD]$. Prove that $(BF)$ is the bisector of $\widehat{AFC}$.
