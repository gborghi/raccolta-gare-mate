*Bus route distance from terminal to terminal*

> A motorcyclist travels at constant speed along the bus route connecting terminal A to terminal B, following the regular bus line that links the two terminals. She left terminal A heading toward the other terminal, and noticed that every 3 minutes she passed a bus stop. It took her 45 minutes to reach terminal B. She knows that the distance between the terminal and the stop nearest to it, and between any two consecutive stops, is 2 km. What is the distance between the two terminals?

>

> a) Complete the table above with the results from another game in which Beto played first, Carlos second, and Ana last.

> $$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|}\hline & \text{Ana's score} & \text{Beto's score} & \text{Carlos's score} \\\hline & & 6 & \\\hline & & & 5 \\\hline 5 & & & \\\hline \end{array}$$

*Diamantino's digit-sum reduction game: trace, max 3-digit, count under 2012*

> Diamantino plays with numbers of two or more digits, transforming them into single-digit numbers in the following way: he adds the two rightmost digits and replaces those two digits with the result, repeating the process until he gets what he wants. For example, starting from $367$ he writes $313$, then writes $34$, and finishes writing $7$.
> 
> a) Starting with the number $2012$, what number will Diamantino obtain?
> 
> b) What is the largest three-digit number that Diamantino can transform into $1$?
> 
> c) Diamantino wrote all numbers less than $2012$, with two or more digits, that can be transformed into $9$. How many numbers did he write?
