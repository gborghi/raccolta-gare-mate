*Triangle, incircle tangency construction; prove two lines parallel*

> Let $ABC$ be a triangle with incircle $\omega$. Points $D$ and $E$ lie on sides $AB$ and $AC$ respectively (not at the endpoints), and $\omega$ is also tangent to line $DE$, touching it at a point $P$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of arc $BC$ of the circumcircle, and let $Q$ be the second intersection of line $MP$ with $\omega$. Let $B'$ be the second intersection of line $DM$ with $\omega$ and $C'$ the second intersection of line $EM$ with $\omega$, with $B' \ne C'$, and suppose $A$ and $Q$ lie on the same side of line $BC$. Let $O$ be the incenter of triangle $DEM$, and let $Q'$ be the point on $\omega$ with $B'Q' : C'Q' = BQ : CQ$. Prove that line $MO$ is parallel to line $QQ'$. (Here $XY$ denotes the length of segment $XY$.)

*n x n grid, 2x2 operation from one black cell; find all n fully colorable*

> Let $n$ be an integer with $n \ge 2$, and consider an $n \times n$ grid of cells. Initially exactly one cell in the top row is colored black, and all other cells are white. The following operation may be repeated: choose a $2 \times 2$ block of cells in which exactly one cell is currently colored black, and color the remaining $3$ cells of that block black. Determine all $n$ for which it is possible, by repeating this operation, to reach a state in which every cell of the grid is black.

*n students, n races, badge (a,b) for exactly a top-b finishes; maximize total score*

> Let $n$ be a positive integer. Each of $n$ students runs in $n$ races. In each race all $n$ students are assigned distinct ranks (no two students share a rank). For a pair of positive integers $(a, b)$, a student is said to hold the title $(a, b)$ if the student finishes among the top $b$ in exactly $a$ of the $n$ races. A student's score is defined as the maximum value of $a - b$ over all titles $(a, b)$ that the student holds. Determine the maximum possible value of the sum of the scores of all $n$ students.

*Sequence with max-min spread 1 over any p+1 and any q+1 consecutive terms*

> Let $p$ and $q$ be distinct, mutually coprime positive integers. Determine all sequences of positive integers $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ that satisfy both of the following conditions: (i) for every positive integer $n$, the difference between the largest and the smallest of $a_n, a_{n+1}, \ldots, a_{n+p}$ equals $1$; (ii) for every positive integer $n$, the difference between the largest and the smallest of $a_n, a_{n+1}, \ldots, a_{n+q}$ equals $1$.
