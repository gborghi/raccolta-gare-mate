*The line of circumcenters O1O2 passes through the second intersection N*

> Given a trapezoid $ABCD$ ($AD \parallel BC$) with $\angle ABC > 90°$. Point $M$ is chosen on the lateral side $AB$. Let $O_1$ and $O_2$ be the circumcenters of the triangles $MAD$ and $MBC$ respectively. The circumcircles of the triangles $MO_1D$ and $MO_2C$ meet again at the point $N$. Prove that the line $O_1O_2$ passes through the point $N$.

*Odd n admitting a permutation where n divides one of a_k squared minus a_{k+1} plus or minus 1*

> Find all odd positive integers $n > 1$ such that there is a permutation $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ of the numbers $1, 2, \ldots, n$, where for each $k$ with $1 \le k \le n$, $n$ divides one of the numbers $a_k^2 - a_{k+1} - 1$ and $a_k^2 - a_{k+1} + 1$ (we assume $a_{n+1} = a_1$).

**Topic:** [[topic_aritmetica|Aritmetica / Teoria dei Numeri]]

**Area:** [[Aritmetica e Teoria dei Numeri|Arithmetic and number theory]]

*An inequality in four positive reals of product 1*

> Let $a, b, c, d > 0$ and $abcd = 1$. Prove that
>
> $$\frac{(a-1)(c+1)}{(1+bc)(1+c)} + \frac{(b-1)(d+1)}{(1+cd)(1+d)} + \frac{(c-1)(a+1)}{(1+da)(1+a)} + \frac{(d-1)(b+1)}{(1+ab)(1+b)} \ge 0.$$

**Topic:** [[topic_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]]
**Metodo:** [[method_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze classiche]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]]
**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi]]
**Fonte:** apri PDF
