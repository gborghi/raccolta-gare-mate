> AN AFFECTIONATE FATHER (Michelangelo Sabatini)
> Gru leaves his villain career to become a full-time father. Every evening he tells a story to his three girls. The probability that the fisherman catches any one salmon that passes in front of him is $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Solomon is seventh in the line and his brothers are the second and the fourth.
