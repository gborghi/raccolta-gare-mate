*Prove two disjoint index sets give equal unions of subsets*

> Assume that $n$ is a positive integer, and $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_{n+1}$ are $n+1$ nonempty subsets of the set $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$. Prove that there are two disjoint and nonempty subsets $\{i_1, i_2, \ldots, i_k\}$ and $\{j_1, j_2, \ldots, j_m\}$ such that
> $$A_{i_1} \cup A_{i_2} \cup \cdots \cup A_{i_k} = A_{j_1} \cup A_{j_2} \cup \cdots \cup A_{j_m}.$$
> (posed by Pan Chengbiao)

*Find all integer n-tuples satisfying sum and sum-of-squares conditions*

> Assume that $n$ is a given positive integer. Find all of the integer groups $(a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n)$ satisfying the conditions:
> (1) $a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_n \ge n^2$;
> (2) $a_1^2 + a_2^2 + \cdots + a_n^2 \le n^3 + 1$.
> (posed by Pan Chengbiao)

*Fibonacci-type sequence: prove recurrence and find divisibility pairs*

> Assume that $\alpha$, $\beta$ are two roots of the equation $x^2 - x - 1 = 0$. Let $a_n = \dfrac{\alpha^n - \beta^n}{\alpha - \beta}$, $n = 1, 2, \ldots$
> (1) Prove that for any positive integer $n$, we have $a_{n+2} = a_{n+1} + a_n$.
> (2) Find all positive integers $a$ and $b$, $a < b$, satisfying that $b$ divides $a_n - 2a \cdot n^b$ for any positive integer $n$. (posed by Li Shenghong)

*Successione di tipo Fibonacci: dimostra la ricorrenza e trova le coppie di divisibilità*

> Si supponga che $\alpha$, $\beta$ siano due radici dell'equazione $x^2 - x - 1 = 0$. Siano $a_n = \dfrac{\alpha^n - \beta^n}{\alpha - \beta}$, $n = 1, 2, \ldots$. (1) Si dimostri che, per ogni intero positivo $n$, si ha $a_{n+2} = a_{n+1} + a_n$. (2) Si determinino tutti gli interi positivi $a$ e $b$, $a < b$, tali che $b$ divida $a_n - 2a \cdot n^b$ per ogni intero positivo $n$. (proposto da Li Shenghong)
