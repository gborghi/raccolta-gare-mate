*Point D on side BC with AD>BC; choose E on AC so AE/EC=BD/(AD-BC); prove AD>BE.*

> Point $D$ is taken on the side $BC$ of a triangle $ABC$ such that $AD > BC$. Let $E$ be a point on the side $AC$ such that $\frac{AE}{EC} = \frac{BD}{AD - BC}$. Show that $AD > BE$.

*S(x)=digit sum; show there exist n_1<...<n_50 with all n_i+S(n_i) equal.*

> Let $S(x)$ denote the sum of the digits of $x$. Show that there exist positive integers $n_1 < n_2 < \cdots < n_{50}$ such that
> $$n_1 + S(n_1) = n_2 + S(n_2) = \cdots = n_{50} + S(n_{50}).$$

*Find all n>=2 for which the cyclic system x_i^2+x_{i+1}^2+50=16x_i+12x_{i+1} has an integer solution.*

*For integers a_i,b_i prove sum over i<j of (|a_i-a_j|+|b_i-b_j|) <= sum over all i,j of |a_i-b_j|.*

*Convex hexagon ABCDEF with angle A+C+E=360 deg and (AB/BC)(CD/DE)(EF/FA)=1; prove (AB/BF)(FD/DE)(EC/CA)=1.*

> A convex hexagon $ABCDEF$ satisfies
> $$\angle A + \angle C + \angle E = 360^\circ \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{AB}{BC} \cdot \frac{CD}{DE} \cdot \frac{EF}{FA} = 1.$$
> Prove that
> $$\frac{AB}{BF} \cdot \frac{FD}{DE} \cdot \frac{EC}{CA} = 1.$$

**Metodo:** [[method_trigonometria|Trigonometria]], [[method_simmetria|Simmetria]]
