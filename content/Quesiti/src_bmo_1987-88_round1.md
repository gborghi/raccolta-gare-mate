*Find unique integer pair r, n with 0<n<200 satisfying two fraction inequalities*

> Find a pair of integers $r$, $n$ such that $0 < n < 200$ and
> $$\frac{r}{n} < \frac{51}{80} < \frac{r+1}{n}, \qquad \frac{r}{n+1} < \frac{51}{80} < \frac{r+1}{n+1}.$$
> Also prove that there is exactly one such pair $r$, $n$.

*Prove recursively defined d(n,m) are always integers*

> Numbers $d(n, m)$ with $m$, $n$ integers, $0 \le m \le n$, are defined by
> $$d(n, 0) = d(n, n) = 1 \quad \text{for all } n \ge 0$$
> and
> $$m\,d(n, m) = m\,d(n-1, m) + (2n - m)\,d(n-1, m-1)$$
> for $0 < m < n$. Prove that all the $d(n, m)$ are integers.

**Topic:** [[topic_algebra|Algebra]], [[topic_combinatoria|Combinatoria]]
**Metodo:** [[method_induzione|Induzione]], [[method_ricorsione|Ricorsione]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_riconoscimento_pattern|Riconoscimento di pattern]]
**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi]], [[Combinatoria, Logica e Probabilita]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11guDhXvMdprlP2g-JECuN_zy0XPTSGKa/view)


<span class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></span>


*Prove recursively defined d(n,m) sono sempre numeri interi*

> I numeri $d(n, m)$ con $m$, $n$ enti, $0 \le m \le n$, sono definiti da $$d(n, 0) = d(n, n) = 1 \quad \text{for all } n \ge 0$$ e $$m\,d(n, m) = m\,d(n-1, m) + (2n - m)\,d(n-1, m-1)$$ per $0 < m < n$. Provare che tutti i $d(n, m)$ sono numeri interi.

*Show least positive value of (x²+y²)/y subject to quadratic constraint is 1/2*

> Show that the least positive value of
> $$\frac{x^2 + y^2}{y},$$
> where $x$, $y$ are real numbers such that
> $$7x^2 + 3xy + 3y^2 = 1,$$
> is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

**Topic:** [[topic_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]], [[topic_algebra|Algebra]]
**Metodo:** [[method_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_stima|Stima]]
**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi]]
**Risposta:** \frac{1}{2}
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11guDhXvMdprlP2g-JECuN_zy0XPTSGKa/view)
