*Maximize cyclic sum of sixth roots over sequences in (0,1)*

> Let $n$ ($\ge 2$) be a positive integer and $a_1$, $a_2$, $\cdots$, $a_n \in (0, 1)$. Find the maximum value of the sum
> $$\sum_{i=1}^{n} \sqrt[6]{a_i(1 - a_{i+1})}$$
> where $a_{n+1} = a_1$.

**Metodo:** [[method_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]], [[method_estremalita|Estremalità]]

**Risposta:** $\dfrac{n}{\sqrt[3]{2}}$

*Smallest k so that product of two quadratics has a real root*

> Find the smallest positive number $k$ such that for any four given distinct real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$ and $d$, each greater than or equal to $k$, there exists a permutation $p$, $q$, $r$ and $s$ of $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ such that the equation
> $$(x^2 + px + q)(x^2 + rx + s) = 0$$
> has at least one real root.

**Metodo:** [[method_casework|Casework]], [[method_estremalita|Estremalità]]

**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi]]
**Risposta:** 4
**Fonte:** [apri libro PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1A6F-Sk42BaQt80ts_sFXZiDY4uHJtL3I/view)

*Il più piccolo k tale che il prodotto di due polinomi di secondo grado abbia una radice reale*

> Trovare il più piccolo numero positivo $k$ tale che, per qualsiasi insieme di quattro numeri reali distinti dati $a$, $b$, $c$ e $d$, ciascuno dei quali è maggiore o uguale a $k$, esista una permutazione $p$, $q$, $r$ e $s$ di $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ tale che l'equazione
> $$(x^2 + px + q)(x^2 + rx + s) = 0$$
> abbia almeno una radice reale.

*Geometry: angle bisector and tan value in circumcircle configuration*

> In $\triangle ABC$, $\angle ABC = 60^\circ$. The tangent at point $P$ to the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ meets line $CB$ extended at $A$. Let $\angle PCB = \alpha$. Points $D$ and $E$ lie on the line segment $PA$ and the circumcircle respectively, such that $\angle DBE = 90^\circ$ and $PD = PE$. Let $F$ be the intersection of $BE$ and $PC$. It is given that lines $AF$, $BP$ and $CD$ are concurrent.
> (1) Prove that $BF$ is the bisector of $\angle PBC$.
> (2) Find the value of $\tan\alpha$.

*Prove fractional parts of sqrt(a) summed are irrational*

> Assume that $a$ is a positive integer and not a perfect square. Prove that for any positive integer $n$, the sum
> $$S_n = \{\sqrt{a}\} + \{\sqrt{a}\}^2 + \cdots + \{\sqrt{a}\}^n$$
> is irrational, where $\{x\} = x - [x]$ and $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$.

*Min size of X so a subset Y of size n hits each 2-element set at most once*

> Given a positive integer $n \geq 2$, let $B_1, B_2, \cdots, B_n$ denote $n$ arbitrary subsets of set $X$, each of which contains exactly two elements. Find the minimum value of $|X|$ such that there exists a subset $Y$ of $X$ satisfying:
> (a) $|Y| = n$;
> (b) $|Y \cap B_i| \leq 1$ for $i = 1, 2, \cdots, n$.
