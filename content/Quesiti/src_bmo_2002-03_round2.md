*Largest prime factor triples in arithmetic progression*

> For each integer $n > 1$, let $p(n)$ denote the largest prime factor of $n$. Determine all triples $(x, y, z)$ of distinct positive integers satisfying:
> (i) $x, y, z$ are in arithmetic progression, and
> (ii) $p(x), p(y), p(z)$ are in arithmetic progression.

*Permutation of positive integers and monotone arithmetic progressions*

> Let $f$ be a permutation of the set $\mathbb{N}$ of all positive integers.
> 
> (i) Show that there is an arithmetic progression of positive integers $a, a+d, a+2d$ (where $d > 0$) such that
> $$f(a) < f(a+d) < f(a+2d).$$
> (ii) Must there be an arithmetic progression $a, a+d, a+2d, \ldots, a+2003d$ (where $d > 0$) such that
> $$f(a) < f(a+d) < f(a+2d) < \cdots < f(a+2003d)?$$

*Permutazione di interi positivi e progressioni aritmetiche monotone*

> Sia $f$ una permutazione dell'insieme $\mathbb{N}$ di tutti gli interi positivi.
>
> (i) Mostrare che esiste una progressione aritmetica di interi positivi $a, a+d, a+2d$ (dove $d > 0$) tale che
> $$f(a) < f(a+d) < f(a+2d).$$
> (ii) Deve necessariamente esistere una progressione aritmetica $a, a+d, a+2d, \ldots, a+2003d$ (dove $d > 0$) tale che
> $$f(a) < f(a+d) < f(a+2d) < \cdots < f(a+2003d)?$$

*Count values less than 2003 in image of f with f(f(n))=n+2003*

> Let $f$ be a function from $\mathbb{N}$ to $\mathbb{N}$ (where $\mathbb{N}$ denotes the set of positive integers) such that $f(f(n)) = n + 2003$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$. How many values less than $2003$ are there in the image of $f$?
