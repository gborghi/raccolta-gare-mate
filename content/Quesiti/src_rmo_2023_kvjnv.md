*Triangle ABC with angle ACB=120°, bisector meets sides, prove KLN equilateral*

> Given a triangle $ABC$ with $\angle ACB = 120^\circ$. The point $L$ is marked on the side $\overline{AB}$ so that $CL$ is the bisector of $\angle ACB$. The points $N$ and $K$ are marked on the sides $AC$ and $BC$, respectively, so that $CN + CK = CL$. Prove that the triangle $KLN$ is equilateral.

**Metodo:** [[method_trigonometria|Trigonometria]]

*Triangolo ABC con angolo ACB=120°, bisettore incontra lati, prova KLN equilaterale*

> Date un triangolo $ABC$ con $\angle ACB = 120^\circ$. Il punto $L$ è segnato sul lato $\overline{AB}$ in modo che $CL$ sia il bisector di $\angle ACB$. I punti $N$ e $K$ sono segnati rispettivamente sui lati $AC$ e $BC$, in modo che $CN + CK = CL$. Prove che il triangolo $KLN$ è equilaterale.

*Polynomial with real coefficients degree 2, pairwise distinct reals a,b,c with f(a)=bc; find f(a+b+c)*

> Let $f(x)$ be a polynomial with real coefficients of degree 2. Suppose that for some pairwise distinct real numbers $a, b, c$ we have
> $$f(a) = bc; \quad f(b) = ca; \quad f(c) = ab.$$
> Determine $f(a + b + c)$ in terms of $a, b, c$.

*Set X of N four-digit numbers from digits 1-8, any two digits share a number in X, find smallest N*

> The set $X$ of $N$ four-digit numbers formed from the digits $1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8$ satisfies the following condition:

> \textit{for any two different digits from $1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8$ there exists a number in $X$ which contains both of them.}

*Triangle sides a,b,c with T_n expression, given ratio condition find all perimeters*

> The side-lengths $a$, $b$, $c$ of a triangle $ABC$ are positive integers. Let
> $$T_n = (a + b + c)^{2n} - (a - b + c)^{2n} - (a + b - c)^{2n} + (a - b - c)^{2n}$$
> for any positive integer $n$. If $\dfrac{T_2}{2T_1} = 2023$ and $a > b > c$, determine all possible perimeters of the triangle $ABC$.

**Topic:** [[topic_algebra|Algebra]], [[topic_aritmetica|Aritmetica / Teoria dei Numeri]]
**Metodo:** [[method_fattorizzazione|Fattorizzazione]], [[method_telescoping|Telescoping]], [[method_casework|Casework]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_riconoscimento_pattern|Riconoscimento di pattern]], [[skill_conteggio_sistematico|Conteggio sistematico]]

**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1O-mEwq-hxsjqhreSHfn8FWGxy_mcCOEX/view)

Se $\dfrac{T_2}{2T_1} = 2023$ e $a > b > c$, determinare tutti i possibili perimetri del triangolo $ABC$.
