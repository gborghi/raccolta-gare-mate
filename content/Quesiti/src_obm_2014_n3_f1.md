*Minimum number of key presses on a restricted operation-first calculator to reach 100*

> On a very simple calculator it is not possible to press two digits without pressing one of the operations $+$, $-$, $\times$ or $\div$ between the pressed digits. When a digit is pressed, the calculator performs the operation immediately. The calculator starts with $0$ on the display and the first key pressed must be an operation. That is, first one presses an operation, then a digit, then an operation, and so on. For example, one way to make $29$ appear on the display is to press $\boxed{+}$ and then $\boxed{7}$, making $0 + 7 = 7$ appear on the display; then press $\boxed{\times}$ and $\boxed{5}$, obtaining $7 \times 5 = 35$ on the display, and finish by pressing $\boxed{-}$ and $\boxed{6}$, giving as result $35 - 6 = 29$. Thus, it is possible to obtain $29$ with $6$ key presses. Pedro wants the number $100$ to appear on the display. What is the minimum number of key presses, counting operations and digits, that Pedro must make on the calculator?
> (A) $2$ (B) $4$ (C) $6$ (D) $8$ (E) $10$

*Numero minimo di pressioni dei tasti su una calcolatrice a operazione prima restrittiva per raggiungere 100*

> Su una calcolatrice molto semplice, non è possibile premere due cifre senza premere una delle operazioni $+$, $-$, $\times$ o $\div$ tra le cifre premute. Quando si preme una cifra, la calcolatrice esegue immediatamente l'operazione. La calcolatrice parte con $0$ sul display e il primo tasto premuto deve essere un'operazione. Cioè, prima si preme un'operazione, poi una cifra, poi un'operazione, e così via. Ad esempio, un modo per far apparire $29$ sul display è premere $\boxed{+}$ e poi $\boxed{7}$, facendo apparire $0 + 7 = 7$ sul display; poi premere $\boxed{\times}$ e $\boxed{5}$, ottenendo $7 \times 5 = 35$ sul display, e concludere premendo $\boxed{-}$ e $\boxed{6}$, ottenendo come risultato $35 - 6 = 29$. Pertanto, è possibile ottenere $29$ con $6$ pressioni di tasti. Pedro vuole che il numero $100$ appaia sul display. Qual è il numero minimo di pressioni di tasti, contando sia le operazioni sia le cifre, che Pedro deve effettuare sulla calcolatrice?
> (A) $2$ (B) $4$ (C) $6$ (D) $8$ (E) $10$

> In Portugal, October $4$, $1582$ was the last day of the Julian calendar, which was replaced by the calendar currently adopted, the Gregorian calendar. The only difference between the calendars is that, in the Julian calendar, all years multiple of $4$ were leap years; in the Gregorian calendar, the years that are multiples of $100$, but not of $400$, are not leap years. Thus, $1900$ would be a leap year in the Julian calendar, but not in the Gregorian calendar. What day would today, June $3$, $2014$, be if we had not changed the calendar?

*Probability a 3x4 rectangular coin lands fully inside a 10x20 rectangular tile*

> To calculate the probability that a coin of radius $r$ lands totally inside a tile formed of square tiles of side $\ell$, we compute the probability that its center lands inside a smaller square of side $\ell - 2r$ (we remove a "border" of size $r$ from the sides of the square). That probability is equal to $\left(\frac{\ell - 2r}{\ell}\right)^2$. In Esmeralandia, the coins are rectangular. What is the probability that a coin with sides $3$ and $4$ lands totally inside a tile formed by rectangles with sides $10$ and $20$?
> (A) Less than $0.375$. (B) Exactly $0.375$. (C) More than $0.375$ and less than $0.595$. (D) Exactly $0.595$. (E) More than $0.595$.
