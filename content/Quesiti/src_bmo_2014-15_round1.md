*Order five tower-exponential numbers by size*

> Place the following numbers in increasing order of size, and justify your reasoning:
> $$3^{3^4},\quad 3^{4^3},\quad 3^{4^4},\quad 4^{3^3}\quad\text{and}\quad 4^{3^4}.$$

*Pythagorean triple with prime; prove divisibility and perfect square*

> Positive integers $p$, $a$ and $b$ satisfy the equation $p^2 + a^2 = b^2$. Prove that if $p$ is a prime greater than $3$, then $a$ is a multiple of $12$ and $2(p + a + 1)$ is a perfect square.

*Prove t_n = x^n + x^{-n} is integer; find n where t divides t_n*

> Let $x$ be a real number such that $t = x + x^{-1}$ is an integer greater than $2$. Prove that $t_n = x^n + x^{-n}$ is an integer for all positive integers $n$. Determine the values of $n$ for which $t$ divides $t_n$.

*Cyclic quadrilateral ABCD; prove PQ parallel to AB*

> Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic quadrilateral. Let $F$ be the midpoint of the arc $AB$ of its circumcircle which does not contain $C$ or $D$. Let the lines $DF$ and $AC$ meet at $P$ and the lines $CF$ and $BD$ meet at $Q$. Prove that the lines $PQ$ and $AB$ are parallel.

*Find all functions preserving the Egyptian fraction equation 1/a+1/b=1/c*

> Determine all functions $f(n)$ from the positive integers to the positive integers which satisfy the following condition: whenever $a$, $b$ and $c$ are positive integers such that $\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} = \frac{1}{c}$, then
> $$\frac{1}{f(a)} + \frac{1}{f(b)} = \frac{1}{f(c)}.$$

*Ricerca tutte le funzioni che preservano l'equazione di frazione egiziana 1/a+1/b=1/c*

> Determinare tutte le funzioni $f(n)$ dai numeri interi positivi ai numeri interi positivi che soddisfano la seguente condizione: quando $a$, $b$ e $c$ sono numeri interi positivi come $\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} = \frac{1}{c}$, allora $$\frac{1}{f(a)} + \frac{1}{f(b)} = \frac{1}{f(c)}.$$
