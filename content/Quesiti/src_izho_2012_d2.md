*Existence of f with f(f(x)) = 2f(x)-x-2 and integers m ≤ n = f(m)*

> Do there exist integers $m$, $n$ and a function $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ satisfying both of the following?
>
> (i) $f(f(x)) = 2f(x)-x-2$ for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$;
>
> (ii) $m \le n$ and $f(m) = n$.

**Metodo:** [[method_casework|Analisi per casi]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_riconoscimento_pattern|Pattern recognition]]
**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi|Algebra and analysis]]
**Fonte:** apri PDF

>
> (i) $f(f(x)) = 2f(x)-x-2$ per ogni $x \in \mathbb{R}$;
>
> (ii) $m \le n$ e $f(m) = n$.

*Angle sum in a quadrilateral with equilateral triangles on the diagonals*

> Equilateral triangles $ACB'$ and $BDC'$ are drawn on the diagonals of a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ so that $B$ and $B'$ lie on the same side of $AC$, and $C$ and $C'$ lie on the same side of $BD$. Given that $B'C' = AB+CD$, find $\angle BAD + \angle CDA$.

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]]
**Metodo:** [[method_simmetria|Sfruttamento della simmetria]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_ragionamento_geometrico|Ragionamento geometrico]]

**Fonte:** apri PDF
