*Find all positive functions with f(xf(y))=yf(x) tending to 0*

> Find all functions $f$ defined on the set of positive real numbers which take positive values and satisfy the conditions:
> 
> (i) $f(xf(y)) = yf(x)$ for all positive $x, y$;
> 
> (ii) $f(x) \to 0$ as $x \to \infty$.

*Trovare tutte le funzioni positive f tali che f(xf(y))=yf(x) tenda a 0*

>
> (i) $f(xf(y)) = yf(x)$ per ogni $x, y$ positivo;

> (ii) $f(x) \to 0$ quando $x \to \infty$.

*Prove angle O1AO2 equals angle M1AM2 for two circles*

> Let $A$ be one of the two distinct points of intersection of two unequal coplanar circles $C_1$ and $C_2$ with centers $O_1$ and $O_2$, respectively. One of the common tangents to the circles touches $C_1$ at $P_1$ and $C_2$ at $P_2$, while the other touches $C_1$ at $Q_1$ and $C_2$ at $Q_2$. Let $M_1$ be the midpoint of $P_1Q_1$ and $M_2$ be the midpoint of $P_2Q_2$. Prove that $\angle O_1AO_2 = \angle M_1AM_2$.

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]]
**Metodo:** [[method_trigonometria|Tecniche trigonometriche]], [[method_coordinate|Coordinate method]], [[method_simmetria|Using symmetry]]

**Fonte:** [apri PDF p.1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qHrtan02NnpLf3E60-PekLeYdH4Y9tnD/view)

*Dimostrare che l'angolo O1AO2 è uguale all'angolo M1AM2 per due circonferenze*

Una delle tangenti comuni alle circonferenze tocca $C_1$ in $P_1$ e $C_2$ in $P_2$, mentre l'altra tocca $C_1$ in $Q_1$ e $C_2$ in $Q_2$. Sia $M_1$ il punto medio di $P_1Q_1$ e sia $M_2$ il punto medio di $P_2Q_2$. Dimostrare che $\angle O_1AO_2 = \angle M_1AM_2$.

*Largest integer not representable as nonnegative combination (Frobenius)*

> Let $a, b, c$ be positive integers, no two of which have a common divisor greater than $1$. Show that $2abc - ab - bc - ca$ is the largest integer which cannot be expressed in the form $xbc + yca + zab$ where $x, y, z$ are non-negative integers.

**Topic:** [[topic_aritmetica|Arithmetic / Number theory]]
**Metodo:** [[method_congruenze|Modular arithmetic / congruences]], [[method_casework|Case analysis]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_casework_accurato|Careful case analysis]], [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Algebraic manipulation]]

**Fonte:** [apri PDF p.1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qHrtan02NnpLf3E60-PekLeYdH4Y9tnD/view)

*Two-coloring of equilateral triangle edges yields right triangle*

> Let $ABC$ be an equilateral triangle and $\mathcal{E}$ the set of all points contained in the three segments $AB$, $BC$ and $CA$ (including $A$, $B$ and $C$). Determine whether, for every partition of $\mathcal{E}$ into two disjoint subsets, at least one of the two subsets contains the vertices of a right-angled triangle. Justify your answer.

> Let $a, b, c$ be the lengths of the sides of a triangle. Prove that:

> $$a^2b(a-b) + b^2c(b-c) + c^2a(c-a) \geq 0.$$
> 
> Determine when equality occurs.
