*Prove a ratio sum is constant for point in triangle*

> Through a point $P$ in the interior of a fixed triangle $ABC$ lines $PL$, $PM$, $PN$ are drawn parallel to the medians through $A$, $B$, $C$ respectively to meet $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ at $L$, $M$, $N$ respectively. Prove that
> $$\frac{BL}{BC} + \frac{CM}{CA} + \frac{AN}{AB}$$
> is constant (independent of $P$).

*Root of real equation lies in given interval*

> The real number $t$ is a root of the equation
> $$x^n + a_1 x^{n-1} + \cdots + a_{n-1} x + a_n = 0, \quad (n \geq 2),$$
> where the coefficients are real and satisfy $-1 \leq a_k \leq 1$, $(2 \leq k \leq n)$.
> 
> Prove that
> $$-(1 + \sqrt{5}) \leq t \leq \frac{1}{2}(1 + \sqrt{5}).$$

*Quadratic form has only trivial integer solution; cubic has infinitely many*

> Prove that the equation $x^2 - 3y^2 - 3z^2 + 7t^2 = 0$ has no solutions in integers other than $x = y = z = t = 0$.
> 
> Prove that $x^3 - 3y^3 - 3z^3 + 7t^3 = 0$ has infinitely many solutions in positive integers $x_0, y_0, z_0, t_0$ in no two of which the ratio $x:y:z:t$ is the same.

*Figure-of-eight curve with two hexagons properties*

> A 'figure-of-eight' curve, $S$, consists of two touching circles of equal radii. Show that a pair of two distinct congruent hexagons (not necessarily convex) exists with the following properties:
> 
> (a) All the vertices of the hexagons lie on $S$.
> 
> (b) Each hexagon has all its vertices on one circle.
> 
> (c) Neither hexagon can be obtained from the other by a single translation, a single rotation or a single reflection.

*Figura di otto curve con due proprietà esagoniche*

> Una curva di "figure di otto", $S$, è costituita da due cerchi che si toccano di uguali radii. Mostrare che esiste una coppia di due esagoni congruenti distinti (non necessariamente convexi) con le seguenti proprietà:
> 
> (a) Tutti i vertici degli esagoni si trovano su $S$.
> 
> b) Ogni esagono ha tutti i suoi vertici su un cerchio.
> 
> (c) Nessun esagono può essere ottenuto dall'altro con una singola traduzione, una singola rotazione o un singolo riflesso.
