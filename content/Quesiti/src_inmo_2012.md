*Sequence of functions defined recursively; prove each is polynomial with integer coefficients*

> Define a sequence $\{f_0(x), f_1(x), f_2(x), \ldots\}$ of functions by
> $$f_0(x) = 1, \quad f_1(x) = x, \quad (f_n(x))^2 - 1 = f_{n+1}(x)f_{n-1}(x), \quad \text{for } n \ge 1.$$
> Prove that each $f_n(x)$ is a polynomial with integer coefficients.

*Count 'good' interior points of triangle yielding 27 equal-area sub-triangles*

> Let $ABC$ be a triangle. An interior point $P$ of $ABC$ is said to be **good** if we can find exactly 27 rays emanating from $P$ intersecting the sides of the triangle $ABC$ such that the triangle is divided by these rays into 27 smaller triangles of equal area. Determine the number of **good** points for a given triangle $ABC$.

*Functional equation on integers: find all f values and set where f(n)≠0*

> Let $f : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$ be a function satisfying $f(0) \neq 0$, $f(1) = 0$ and
> \begin{itemize}
> \item[(i)] $f(xy) + f(x)f(y) = f(x) + f(y)$;
> \item[(ii)] $(f(x-y) - f(0))f(x)f(y) = 0$,
> \end{itemize}
> for all $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}$, simultaneously.
> \begin{enumerate}
> \item[(a)] Find the set of all possible values of the function $f$.
> \item[(b)] If $f(0) \neq 0$ and $f(2) = 0$, find the set of all integers $n$ such that $f(n) \neq 0$.
> \end{enumerate}

**Metodo:** [[method_casework|Casework]], [[method_induzione|Induzione]]

*Equazione funzionale sui numeri interi: trovare tutti i valori di f e impostare dove f(n) ≠0*

> Che $f : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$ sia una funzione che soddisfi $f(0) \neq 0$, $f(1) = 0$ e \begin{itemize} \item[(i)] $f(xy) + f(x)f(y) = f(x) + f(y)$; \item[(ii) $(f(x-y) - f(0))f(x)f(y) = 0$, \end{itemize} per tutti $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}$, contemporaneamente. \begin{enumerate} \item[(a)] Trova l'insieme di tutti i valori possibili della funzione $f$. \item[(b) Se $f(0) \neq 0$ e $f(2) = 0$, trovare l'insieme di tutti gli integri $n$ tale da $f(n) \neq 0$. \end{enumere}
