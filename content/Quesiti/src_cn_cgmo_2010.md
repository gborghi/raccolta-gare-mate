*Maximum value of sum of intersection ratios over cyclic subsets*

> Let $n$ be an integer greater than two, and let $A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_{2n}$ be pairwise distinct nonempty subsets of $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$. Determine the maximum value of $\sum_{i=1}^{2n} \dfrac{|A_i \cap A_{i+1}|}{|A_i| \cdot |A_{i+1}|}$. (Here, we set $A_{2n+1} = A_1$. For a set $X$, let $|X|$ denote the number of elements in $X$.)

*Prove ED ≥ FD in isosceles triangle with circumcircle configuration*

> In triangle $ABC$, $AB = AC$. Point $D$ is the midpoint of side $BC$. Point $E$ lies outside the circle $ABC$ such that $CE \perp AB$ and $BE = BD$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of segment $BE$. Point $F$ lies on the minor arc $AB$ of the circumcircle of triangle $ABD$ such that $MF \perp BE$. Prove that $ED \ge FD$.

*Prove weighted inequality for real numbers on unit sphere*

> Let $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n$ (where $n \ge 2$) be real numbers with $x_1^2 + x_2^2 + \cdots + x_n^2 = 1$. Prove that
> $$\sum_{k=1}^{n} \left(1 - \frac{k}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} i x_i^2}\right)^2 \cdot \frac{x_k^2}{k} \le \left(\frac{n-1}{n+1}\right)^2 \sum_{k=1}^{n} \frac{x_k^2}{k}.$$
> Determine when the equality holds.

*Maximum size of a set of permutations with no triple all-between property*

> Let $n$ be an integer greater than or equal to 1. For a permutation $p = (p_1, p_2, \ldots, p_n)$ of $(1, 2, \ldots, n)$, we say that $j$ lies between $i$ and $k$ if $i < j < k$ or $k < j < i$. (For example, in the permutation $(1, 3, 2, 4)$, $3$ lies between $1$ and $4$, and $3$ does not lie between $1$ and $2$.) Let $S = \{p^{(1)}, p^{(2)}, \ldots, p^{(m)}\}$ be a set of $m$ distinct permutations of $(1, 2, \ldots, n)$ such that among every three distinct numbers in $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$, one of these numbers does not lie between the other two in every permutation $p \in S$. Determine the maximum value of $m$.

**Metodo:** [[method_estremalita|Estremalità]], [[method_induzione|Induzione]], [[method_casework|Casework]]

**Area:** [[Combinatoria, Logica e Probabilita]]
**Risposta:** $2^{n-1}$
**Fonte:** [apri libro PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1cjmD7lTT4vb3nB3ylcVCHeHucUE2rNI0/view)


<div class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></div>


*Dimensione massima di un insieme di permutazioni privo della proprietà di tripla all-between*

> Sia $n$ un intero maggiore o uguale a 1. Per una permutazione $p = (p_1, p_2, \ldots, p_n)$ di $(1, 2, \ldots, n)$, si dice che $j$ giace tra $i$ e $k$ se $i < j < k$ oppure $k < j < i$. (Ad esempio, nella permutazione $(1, 3, 2, 4)$, $3$ giace tra $1$ e $4$, e $3$ non giace tra $1$ e $2$.) Sia $S = \{p^{(1)}, p^{(2)}, \ldots, p^{(m)}\}$ un insieme di $m$ permutazioni distinte di $(1, 2, \ldots, n)$ tale che, tra ogni tre numeri distinti in $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$, uno di questi numeri non giace tra gli altri due in ogni permutazione $p \in S$. Determinare il valore massimo di $m$.

*Least odd n > 5 expressible as m^2 + k^2 + (k+1)^2 in two ways with distinct terms*
