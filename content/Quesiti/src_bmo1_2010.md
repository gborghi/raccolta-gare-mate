*Find integers x,y,z satisfying two simultaneous equations*

> Find all integers $x$, $y$ and $z$ such that
> $$x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = x + y + 1$$

> $$x + y + z = 4018.$$

*Circle through four points with two parallel chords*

> Points $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ and $E$ lie, in that order, on a circle and the lines $AB$ and $ED$ are parallel. The line through $D$ which is parallel to $AC$ meets $BE$ at $F$. Prove that $BC = DF$.

*BMO exam problem ordering and maximum score*

> Isaac attempts all six questions on an Olympiad paper in order. Each question is marked on a scale from $0$ to $10$. He never scores more in a later question than in any earlier question. How many different possible sequences of six marks are there?

> Two circles, of different radii, with centres at $B$ and $C$, touch externally at $A$. A common tangent, not through $A$, touches the first circle at $D$ and the second at $E$. The line through $A$ which is perpendicular to $DE$ meets $BC$ at $F$. Prove that $BC = 2AF$.

*Treasure map: prove digging in one place suffices*

> Long John Silverman has captured a treasure map from Adam McBones. Adam has buried the treasure at the point $(x, y)$ with integer coordinates (not necessarily positive). He has indicated on the map the values of $x^2 + y$ and $x + y^2$, and these values are both integers. Prove that Long John has to dig in only one place to find the treasure.

**Topic:** [[topic_aritmetica|Aritmetica / Teoria dei Numeri]], [[topic_algebra|Algebra]]
**Metodo:** [[method_fattorizzazione|Fattorizzazione]], [[method_congruenze|Congruenze]]

**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1awOFH4w30UoodeziKpr1Y2H1zYJ0Te28/view)

*Il tesoro è in un solo punto intero, dati $x^2 + y$ e $x + y^2$*

> Long John Silverman ha sottratto una mappa del tesoro ad Adam McBones. Adam ha sepolto il tesoro nel punto $(x, y)$ a coordinate intere (non necessariamente positive). Sulla mappa ha indicato i valori di $x^2 + y$ e di $x + y^2$, ed entrambi i valori sono interi. Dimostrare che Long John deve scavare in un solo posto per trovare il tesoro.
