*Cooking steaks optimally with one pan holding two*

> To prepare a steak, it must be cooked one minute on each side. We have only one pan, in which we can place at most two steaks at a time.
> 
> a) How can we prepare four steaks in four minutes?
> 
> b) How can we prepare five steaks in five minutes?
> 
> (We obviously do not need to count the time lost during the manipulations.)
