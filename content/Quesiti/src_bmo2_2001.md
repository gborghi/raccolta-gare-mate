*Marbles transferred between two players; find q/p in terms of n*

> Ahmed and Beth have $p$ and $q$ marbles, with $p > q$. Starting with Ahmed, each in turn gives to the other as many marbles as the other already possesses. It is found that after $2n$ such transfers, Ahmed has $q$ marbles and Beth has $p$ marbles.
> 
> Find $\frac{q}{p}$ in terms of $n$.

*Triangle with angle bisector and right angle; prove angle equality*

> A triangle $ABC$ has $\angle ACB > \angle ABC$. The internal bisector of $\angle BAC$ meets $BC$ at $D$. The point $E$ on $AB$ is such that $\angle EDB = 90^\circ$. The point $F$ on $AC$ is such that $\angle BDE = \angle CDF$.
> 
> Show that $\angle BAD = \angle FDC$.

*N dwarfs in a circle; find maximum and minimum of sum of height differences*

> $N$ dwarfs of heights $1, 2, \ldots, N$ are arranged in a circle. For each of the $N$ pairs of neighbouring dwarfs, the positive difference between the heights is calculated; the sum of these $N$ differences is called the value $V$ of the arrangement.
> 
> Find (with proof) the maximum and minimum possible values of $V$.

**Metodo:** [[method_estremalita|Estremalità]], [[method_casework|Casework]], [[method_doppio_conteggio|Doppio conteggio]]

*Massimo e minimo della somma $V$ delle differenze di altezza fra nani vicini*

> $N$ nani di altezze $1, 2, \ldots, N$ sono disposti in cerchio. Per ciascuna delle $N$ coppie di nani vicini si calcola la differenza positiva fra le altezze; la somma di queste $N$ differenze si chiama valore $V$ della disposizione.
>
> Trovare (con dimostrazione) il massimo e il minimo valore possibile di $V$.
