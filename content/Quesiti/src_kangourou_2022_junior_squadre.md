*Numero lati n con somma diametrale minima 2022*

> Quanti vertici?

*What is the value of n?*

> How many vertices?
>
> At each of the vertices and at the centre of a regular polygon with n sides (n even) a positive integer has been written. The n + 1 numbers written were chosen so that they were all different from one another and so that, as the pairs of opposite vertices vary (that is, vertices aligned with the centre), the sum of the three aligned numbers (at the two vertices and at the centre) was always the same and as small as possible. On these assumptions that sum turned out to be 2022. What is the value of n?
