> We are going to prove that 4 is greater than 4. Let $a$ and $b$ be two numbers such that $a>4$ and $a=b$.

> 2) We put $-1$ in evidence in the second member of the equation: $a-4=-1(-b+4)$, so $a-4=-1(4-b)$.
