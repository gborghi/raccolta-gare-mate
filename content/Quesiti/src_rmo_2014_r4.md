*Isosceles trapezium incircle: CE equals geometric mean of AB and CD*

> Let $ABCD$ be an isosceles trapezium having an incircle; let $AB$ and $CD$ be the parallel sides and let $CE$ be the perpendicular from $C$ on to $AB$. Prove that $CE$ is equal to the geometric mean of $AB$ and $CD$.

*Incircles and concyclicity: prove B,E,I,F concyclic in acute triangle*

> Let $ABC$ be an acute-angled triangle and let $I$ be its incentre. Let the incircle of triangle $ABC$ touch $BC$ in $D$. The incircle of triangle $ABD$ touches $AB$ in $E$; the incircle of triangle $ACD$ touches $AC$ in $F$. Prove that $B, E, I, F$ are concyclic.
