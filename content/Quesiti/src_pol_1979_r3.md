*Distinct remainders set: divisibility by n*

> Let be given a set $\{r_1, r_2, \ldots, r_n\}$ of natural numbers that give distinct remainders when divided by a natural number $n$. Prove that if $k \ge n/2$, then for every integer $n$ there exist indices $i$ and $j$ (not necessarily distinct) such that $r_i + r_j - n$ is divisible by $n$.

*Probability of exactly k successes in n independent tests*

> An experiment consists of performing $n$ independent tests. The $i$-th test is successful with the probability equal to $p_i$. Let $p_k$ be the probability that exactly $k$ tests succeed. Prove that
> $$\sum_{k=0}^{n} k p_k = \sum_{i=1}^{n} p_i.$$

*Probabilità di esattamente k successi in n prove indipendenti*

> Un esperimento consiste nel condurre $n$ prove indipendenti. La $i$-esima prova ha successo con probabilità pari a $p_i$. Sia $p_k$ la probabilità che esattamente $k$ prove abbiano successo. Dimostrare che
> $$\sum_{k=0}^{n} k p_k = \sum_{i=1}^{n} p_i.$$

*Sequence in [1,A] with ratio bound implies sequence in [1,A]*

> Let $A > 1$ and $B > 1$ be real numbers and $(a_n)$ be a sequence of numbers in the interval $[1, AB]$. Prove that there exists a sequence $(s_n)$ of numbers in the interval $[1, A]$ such that
> $$\frac{a_m}{s_m} \le B \cdot \frac{a_n}{s_n} \quad \text{for all } m, n = 1, 2, \ldots$$
