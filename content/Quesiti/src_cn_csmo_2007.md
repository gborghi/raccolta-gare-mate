*Evaluate a sum of floor values of a minimized expression over k*

> Suppose $a_n = \min\left\{k + \frac{n+1}{k} \mid k \in \mathbb{N}^*\right\}$, find the value of $S_{n^2} = [a_1] + [a_2] + \cdots + [a_{n^2}]$, where $n \ge 2$, and $[x]$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$.

**Topic:** [[topic_algebra|Algebra]], [[topic_aritmetica|Aritmetica / Teoria dei Numeri]]
**Metodo:** [[method_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]], [[method_conteggio|Conteggio]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_stima|Stima]]
**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi]], [[Aritmetica e Teoria dei Numeri]]
**Risposta:** $S_{n^2} = 4m^2 + 3m + 1$
**Fonte:** [apri libro PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1A6F-Sk42BaQt80ts_sFXZiDY4uHJtL3I/view)

> [!warning] Estratto da verificare con la fonte.


<span class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></span>

*Find all prime triples (a,b,c) with 0<a<b<c<100 and a+1,b+1,c+1 in geometric progression*

> Find the array $(a, b, c)$ satisfying conditions as follows:
> 
> (1) $0 < a < b < c < 100$, where $a$, $b$, $c$ are all prime numbers.
> 
> (2) $a + 1$, $b + 1$, $c + 1$ constitute the geometric progression.

*Prove cyclic sum of a^k/(a+b) ≥ 3/2 for abc=1 and integer k≥2*

> Given real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$ such that $abc = 1$, prove that for all integers $k \geqslant 2$,
> $$\frac{a^k}{a+b} + \frac{b^k}{b+c} + \frac{c^k}{c+a} \geqslant \frac{3}{2}.$$
