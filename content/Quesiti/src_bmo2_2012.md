*Cyclic quadrilateral with diagonals meeting at E; midpoints of the four sides; prove the circles EPS and EQR have equal radius.*

> The diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ of a cyclic quadrilateral meet at $E$. The midpoints of the sides $AB$, $BC$, $CD$ and $DA$ are $P$, $Q$, $R$ and $S$ respectively. Prove that the circles $EPS$ and $EQR$ have the same radius.

*Recursively defined function on positive integers using floors of (2n-1)/3 and 2n/3; decide whether f(n)-f(n-1) <= n for all n>1.*

> A function $f$ is defined on the positive integers by $f(1) = 1$ and, for $n > 1$,
> $$f(n) = f\!\left(\left\lfloor \frac{2n-1}{3} \right\rfloor\right) + f\!\left(\left\lfloor \frac{2n}{3} \right\rfloor\right)$$
> where $\lfloor x \rfloor$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$. Is it true that $f(n) - f(n-1) \le n$ for all $n > 1$?
> 
> [Here are some examples of the use of $\lfloor x \rfloor$: $\lfloor \pi \rfloor = 3$, $\lfloor 1729 \rfloor = 1729$ and $\left\lfloor \frac{2012}{1000} \right\rfloor = 2$.]

<div class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></div>


*È vero che $f(n) - f(n-1) \le n$ per ogni $n > 1$?*

> Una funzione $f$ è definita sugli interi positivi da $f(1) = 1$ e, per $n > 1$,
> $$f(n) = f\!\left(\left\lfloor \frac{2n-1}{3} \right\rfloor\right) + f\!\left(\left\lfloor \frac{2n}{3} \right\rfloor\right)$$
> dove $\lfloor x \rfloor$ indica il massimo intero minore o uguale a $x$. È vero che $f(n) - f(n-1) \le n$ per ogni $n > 1$?
>
> [Ecco alcuni esempi d'uso di $\lfloor x \rfloor$: $\lfloor \pi \rfloor = 3$, $\lfloor 1729 \rfloor = 1729$ e $\left\lfloor \frac{2012}{1000} \right\rfloor = 2$.]

*The reals are partitioned into two disjoint subsets; prove that for every pair (m,n) of positive integers there exist x<y<z in the same subset with m(z-y)=n(y-x).*

> The set of real numbers is split into two subsets which do not intersect. Prove that for each pair $(m, n)$ of positive integers, there are real numbers $x < y < z$ all in the same subset such that $m(z-y) = n(y-x)$.

*Show there is a positive integer k such that if m divides a^n+b^n+c^n-d^n-e^n-f^n for all n in [1,k], then m divides it for all positive integers n.*

> Show that there is a positive integer $k$ with the following property: if $a, b, c, d, e$ and $f$ are integers and $m$ is a divisor of
> $$a^n + b^n + c^n - d^n - e^n - f^n$$
> for all integers $n$ in the range $1 \le n \le k$, then $m$ is a divisor of $a^n + b^n + c^n - d^n - e^n - f^n$ for all positive integers $n$.

**Metodo:** [[method_congruenze|Congruenze]], [[method_fattorizzazione|Fattorizzazione]]

**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/101xOLerYbSbN43TPnIwIpRwpKE2owKy-/view)


<div class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></div>


*Un intero positivo $k$ che basta a garantire la divisibilità per ogni $n$*

> Dimostrare che esiste un intero positivo $k$ con la proprietà seguente: se $a, b, c, d, e$ e $f$ sono interi e $m$ è un divisore di
> $$a^n + b^n + c^n - d^n - e^n - f^n$$
> per tutti gli interi $n$ nell'intervallo $1 \le n \le k$, allora $m$ è un divisore di $a^n + b^n + c^n - d^n - e^n - f^n$ per tutti gli interi positivi $n$.
