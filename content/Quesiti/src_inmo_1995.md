*Infinitely many coprime pairs with two quadratics having integer roots*

> Show that there are infinitely many pairs $(a,b)$ of coprime integers such that both the quadratic equations $x^2 + ax + b = 0$ and $x^2 + 2ax + b = 0$ have integer roots.

*Ratio of radii of mixtilinear circle to incircle equals tan^2((B-C)/4)*

> Let $\Gamma$ be the circle lying inside a triangle $ABC$ and touching the sides $AB$ and $AC$ and the incircle $\Gamma'$ of the triangle externally. Show that the ratio of the radii of $\Gamma$ and $\Gamma'$ equals $\tan^2\!\dfrac{B-C}{4}$.

**Metodo:** [[method_trigonometria|Trigonometria]], [[method_coordinate|Coordinate]]

*Real numbers a_k>1 satisfying sum condition, prove double sum inequality*

> The real numbers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ are all greater than $1$ and satisfy $|a_k - a_{k+1}| < 1$ for $1 \le k \le n-1$. Prove that
> $$\frac{a_1}{a_2} + \frac{a_2}{a_3} + \cdots + \frac{a_{n-1}}{a_n} + \frac{a_n}{a_1} < 2n-1.$$

**Topic:** [[topic_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]]
**Metodo:** [[method_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]], [[method_casework|Casework]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_stima|Stima]], [[skill_lettura_attenta|Lettura attenta]]
**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-pje9yn5-Oz-tcMTgQ38tNi0Sa3TrKyD/view)
