*Find all sets of digits appearing an odd number of times in 1 to 2016*

> The integers $1, 2, 3, \ldots, 2016$ are written down in base $10$, appearing one after another. Each of the digits from $0$ to $9$ appears many times. A digit $d$ is called odd if the number of times $d$ appears in the list $1, 2, 3, \ldots, 2016$ is odd. Find all possibilities for the set of odd digits.

*Naomi and Tom integer-sum game; determine winning strategy*

> Naomi and Tom play a game. They take it in turns to choose a positive integer, with Naomi going first. *Equal areas [ACP]=[ABC]=[BQC] imply angle BCA=90 degrees*

> Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $\angle A < \angle B < \angle C$. Let $\Gamma$ be the circle through $A$, $B$ and $C$. The tangents to $\Gamma$ at $A$ and $C$ meet at $P$. The line segment $AP$ meets $\Gamma$ again at $Q$. It is given that
> $$[ACP] = [ABC] = [BQC].$$
> Prove that $\angle BCA = 90^\circ$. Here $[XYZ]$ denotes the area of triangle $XYZ$.

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]]
**Metodo:** [[method_simmetria|Simmetria]], [[method_trigonometria|Trigonometria]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_ragionamento_geometrico|Ragionamento geometrico]], [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_lettura_attenta|Lettura attenta]]
**Area:** [[Geometria]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Iu7Go4fJuQ3wicwoPBKyARAoXj-MZj3T/view)


<div class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></div>


*Dimostrare che $\angle BCA = 90^\circ$*

> Sia $ABC$ un triangolo con $\angle A < \angle B < \angle C$. Sia $\Gamma$ la circonferenza per $A$, $B$ e $C$. Le tangenti a $\Gamma$ in $A$ e in $C$ si incontrano in $P$. Il segmento $AP$ seca di nuovo $\Gamma$ in $Q$. È dato che
> $$[ACP] = [ABC] = [BQC].$$
> Dimostrare che $\angle BCA = 90^\circ$. Qui $[XYZ]$ indica l'area del triangolo $XYZ$.
