*Diameter and perpendiculars in a circle; prove LS = LT*

> Let $ABC$ be a triangle, with an obtuse angle at $A$. Let $Q$ be a point (other than $A$, $B$ or $C$) on the circle through $A$, $B$ and $C$, on the same side of chord $BC$ as $A$, and let $P$ be the other end of the diameter through $Q$. The perpendiculars from $Q$ to $CA$ and $AB$ meet the line $PA$ at $S$ and $T$ respectively. The foot of the perpendicular from $P$ to the line $BC$ is $L$. Show that $LS = LT$.

*Diametro e perpendicolari in una circonferenza; dimostrare che LS = LT*

> Sia $ABC$ un triangolo, con un angolo ottuso in $A$. Sia $Q$ un punto (diverso da $A$, $B$ o $C$) sulla circonferenza passante per $A$, $B$ e $C$, sullo stesso lato della corda $BC$ rispetto a $A$, e sia $P$ l'altra estremità del diametro passante per $Q$. Le perpendicolari condotte da $Q$ a $CA$ e a $AB$ incontrano la retta $PA$ rispettivamente in $S$ e in $T$. Il piede della perpendicolare condotta da $P$ alla retta $BC$ è $L$. Dimostrare che $LS = LT$.

*Interior point of triangle; prove a sine-ratio equality*

> Let $P$ be an internal point of triangle $ABC$ and let $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$ be defined by
> $$\alpha = \angle BPC - \angle BAC,$$
> $$\beta = \angle CPA - \angle CBA,$$
> $$\gamma = \angle APB - \angle ACB.$$
> Prove that
> $$PA \cdot \frac{\sin \angle BAC}{\sin \alpha} = PB \cdot \frac{\sin \angle CBA}{\sin \beta} = PC \cdot \frac{\sin \angle ACB}{\sin \gamma}.$$

*Recurrence on positive integers; prove integer values and count solutions to f(n)=2n*

> The function $f$ is defined on the set of positive integers by $f(1) = 1$, $f(2) = 2$, and
> $$f(n+1) = (1 + (2n + 1)f(n))f(n-1)$$
> for $n \geq 2$.
> 
> (i) Prove that $f(n)$ is always an integer.
> (ii) For how many positive integers less than $2007$ is $f(n) = 2n$?
