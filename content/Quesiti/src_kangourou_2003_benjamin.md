*How many days until the next date divisible by 10001*

> 2003 is a prime number. Today is 20-03-2003: deleting the hyphens that separate the day from the month from the year (and the related spaces), we would write 20032003, and this number is divisible by 10001. How many days will pass before we reach the next date which, by the same rule, would be represented by a number still divisible by 10001?
> A) 365
> B) 366
> C) 396
> D) 397
> E) none of the above
