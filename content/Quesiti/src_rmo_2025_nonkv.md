*Count configurations of n lines with prescribed intersection points*

> (a) Let $n \ge 3$ be an integer. Find a configuration of $n$ lines in the plane which has exactly
> 
> (i) $n-1$ distinct points of intersection;
> 
> (ii) $n$ distinct points of intersection.
> 
> (b) Give configurations of $n$ lines that have exactly $n+1$ distinct points of intersection for (i) $n = 8$ and (ii) $n = 9$.

*Two circles, transversal line, concyclicity and equal lengths*

> Let $\Omega$ and $\Gamma$ be circles centred at $O_1, O_2$ respectively. Suppose that they intersect in points $A, B$. Suppose $O_1$ is outside $\Gamma$ and $O_2$ is outside $\Omega$. Let $\ell$ be a line not passing through $A$ and $B$ that intersects $\Omega$ at $P, R$ and $\Gamma$ at $Q, S$ so that $P, Q, R, S$ lie on the line in this order. Furthermore, the points $O_1, B$ lie on one side of $\ell$ and the points $O_2, A$ lie on the other side of $\ell$. Given that the points $A, P, Q, O_1$ are concyclic and $B, R, S, O_2$ are concyclic, as well prove that $AQ = BR$.

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]]
**Metodo:** [[method_backward|Backward]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_ragionamento_geometrico|Ragionamento geometrico]], [[skill_lettura_attenta|Lettura attenta]]
**Area:** [[Geometria]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ik3u0dWCcvW1PmcS_C8SRI2gnccFRgZA/view)


<div class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></div>


*Due cerchi, retta trasversale, conciclicità e lunghezze uguali*

> Siano $\Omega$ e $\Gamma$ cerchi aventi per centri rispettivamente $O_1, O_2$. Si supponga che essi si intersechino nei punti $A, B$. Si supponga che $O_1$ sia esterno a $\Gamma$ e che $O_2$ sia esterno a $\Omega$. Sia $\ell$ una retta che non passa per $A$ e $B$ e che interseca $\Omega$ in $P, R$ e $\Gamma$ in $Q, S$, in modo che $P, Q, R, S$ giacciano sulla retta in questo ordine. Inoltre, i punti $O_1, B$ giacciono da un lato di $\ell$ e i punti $O_2, A$ giacciono dall'altro lato di $\ell$. Dato che i punti $A, P, Q, O_1$ sono conciclici e i punti $B, R, S, O_2$ sono conciclici, si dimostri inoltre che $AQ = BR$.

*Polynomial with integer coefficients; divisibility by n of p(b)-p(a)*

> Let $p(x)$ be a nonconstant polynomial with integer coefficients, and let $n \ge 2$ be an integer such that the sequence
> $$p(0),\ p(p(0)),\ p(p(p(0))),\ldots$$
> is divisible by $n$. Show that there exist integers $a, b$ such that $0 \le a < b \le n-1$ and $n$ divides $p(b) - p(a)$.
