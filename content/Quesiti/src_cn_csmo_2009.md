*Number of distinct values of weighted sum over all permutations of 1 to n*

> Set the permutation $X = (x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n)$ of $1, 2, \ldots, n$ in $A$. For $\forall X \in A$, let $f(X) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} k x_k$. Let $M = \{f(X) \mid X \in A\}$. Find the value of $|M|$. (Posed by Xiong Bin)

**Topic:** [[topic_combinatoria|Combinatoria]], [[topic_algebra|Algebra]]
**Metodo:** [[method_conteggio|Conteggio]], [[method_induzione|Induzione]], [[method_estremalita|Extremal principle]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_conteggio_sistematico|Conteggio sistematico]], [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_riconoscimento_pattern|Riconoscimento di pattern]]
**Area:** [[Combinatoria, Logica e Probabilita|Combinatorics, logic and probability]], [[Algebra e Analisi|Algebra and analysis]]
**Risposta:** $\frac{n(n^2-1)}{6}+1$
**Fonte:** [apri libro PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1WZGXm4nA5dcZd9asSbnzGZkeWDSjqbF6/view)

*Find max and min of a rational function on the unit simplex*

> Let $f(x, y, z) = \frac{x(2x - y)}{1 + x + 3y} + \frac{y(2y - z)}{1 - y + 3z} + \frac{z(2z - x)}{1 + z + 3x}$, where $x, y, z \ge 0$ and $x + y + z = 1$. Find the maximum value and the minimum value of $f(x, y, z)$. (Posed by Liu Shenglong)

**Metodo:** [[method_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]], [[method_estremalita|Estremalità]], [[method_casework|Casework]]

**Risposta:** $f_{\min}=0,\; f_{\max}=\frac{1}{2}$
