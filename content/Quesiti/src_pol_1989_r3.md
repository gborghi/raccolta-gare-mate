*Three pairwise tangent circles; through a chain of secant constructions prove three points are collinear.*

> Circles $K_1$, $K_2$, $K_3$ are given in the plane such that $K_2$ and $K_3$ are tangent at $P$, $K_3$ and $K_1$ at $Q$, and $K_1$ and $K_2$ at $R$. Lines $PQ$ and $PR$ cut $K_1$ again at $S$ and $T$ respectively. Lines $SR$ and $TQ$ cut $K_2$ and $K_3$ again at $U$ and $V$. Prove that points $P$, $U$, $V$ are collinear.

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]]
**Metodo:** [[method_simmetria|Simmetria]]

*Tre cerchi tangenti in coppia; attraverso una catena di costruzioni secanti dimostrano che tre punti sono collineari.*

> I cerchi $K_1$, $K_2$, $K_3$ sono indicati nel piano in modo tale che $K_2$ e $K_3$ siano tangenti a $P$, $K_3$ e $K_1$ a $Q$, e $K_1$ e $K_2$ a $R$. Le linee $PQ$ e $PR$ si riducono rispettivamente a $S$ e $T$. Le linee $SR$ e $TQ$ sono tagliate $K_2$ e $K_3$ di nuovo a $U$ e $V$. Provare che i punti $P$, $U$, $V$ sono collineari.

*Edges of a cube numbered 1 through 12; prove at least eight index-increasing triples of consecutive edges exist, and exhibit a numbering with exactly eight.*

> The edges of a cube are numbered 1 through 12.
> 
> (a) Prove that for any such numbering there exist at least eight triples of integers $(i, j, k)$ with $1 \le i < j < k \le 12$ such that the edges assigned numbers $i$, $j$, $k$ are consecutive segments of a polygonal line.
> 
> (b) Give an example of a numbering for which there are exactly eight such triples.

*Random chain of subsets starting from an n-set, each a uniformly random subset of the previous; show the expected cardinality of A_k is n/2^k.*

> Let be given positive integers $n$ and $k$. Consider a chain of sets $A_0, A_1, \ldots, A_k$ in which $A_0 = \{1, \ldots, n\}$, and for each $i$, $A_i$ is a randomly chosen subset of $A_{i-1}$ (all choices are equiprobable). Show that the expected cardinality of $A_k$ is $n/2^k$.

**Metodo:** [[method_ricorsione|Ricorsione]], [[method_induzione|Induzione]]
