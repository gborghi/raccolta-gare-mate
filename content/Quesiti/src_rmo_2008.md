*Perpendicular meets and circumradius in acute triangle*

> Let $ABC$ be an acute-angled triangle; let $D$, $F$ be the mid-points of $BC$, $AB$ respectively. Let the perpendicular from $F$ to $AC$ and the perpendicular at $B$ to $BC$ meet in $N$. Prove that $ND$ is the circum-radius of $ABC$.

*Two infinite sequences of positive integers satisfying four conditions*

> Prove that there exist two infinite sequences $(a_n)_{n \ge 1}$ and $(b_n)_{n \ge 1}$ of positive integers such that the following conditions hold simultaneously:
> (i) $1 < a_1 < a_2 < a_3 < \cdots$;
> (ii) $a_n < b_n < a_n^2$, for all $n \ge 1$;
> (iii) $a_n - 1$ divides $b_n - 1$, for all $n \ge 1$;
> (iv) $a_n^2 - 1$ divides $b_n^2 - 1$, for all $n \ge 1$.

**Metodo:** [[method_congruenze|Congruenze]], [[method_induzione|Induzione]]

**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/16mQy3REp8_l6rFZ-_EyE4gi26-48xpHT/view)

*Cubic with all positive real roots: bounds on coefficients*

> Suppose $a$ and $b$ are real numbers such that the roots of the cubic equation $ax^3 - x^2 + bx - 1 = 0$ are all positive real numbers. Prove that:
> (i) $0 < 3ab \le 1$ and (ii) $b \ge \sqrt{3}$.
