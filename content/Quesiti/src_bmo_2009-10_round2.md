*Line up children so each is beside at most two of their friends*

> There are $2010^{2010}$ children at a mathematics camp. Each has at most three friends at the camp, and $A$ is friends with $B$ if and only if $B$ is friends with $A$. The camp leader would like to line the children up so that every child is next to at most two children between any pair of friends. Is it always possible to do this?

*Alineare i bambini in modo che ciascuno sia accanto al massimo a due dei loro amici*

> Ci sono bambini in un campo di matematica. Ciascuno ha al massimo tre amici nel campo, e $A$ è amico di $B$ se e solo se $B$ è amico di $A$. Il capo del campo vorrebbe mettere in fila i bambini in modo che ogni bambino sia vicino a un massimo di due bambini tra qualsiasi coppia di amici. E' sempre possibile farlo?

*Prove angle condition in triangle iff right angle at C*

> In triangle $ABC$, the centroid is $G$ and $D$ is the midpoint of $CA$. The line through $G$ parallel to $BC$ meets $AB$ at $E$. Prove that $\angle AEC = \angle DGC$ if and only if $\angle ACB = 90^\circ$.

*Prove inequality for positive reals x, y, z*

> Prove that, for all positive real numbers $x$, $y$ and $z$,
> $$\left(\frac{x+y}{z}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{y+z}{x}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{z+x}{y}\right)^2 \ge 3(x+y+z)\left(\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}+\frac{1}{z}\right) - 9.$$
