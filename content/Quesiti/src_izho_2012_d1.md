*The area of AH1BH2 does not depend on D on the side AB*

> Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle and let $D$ be an arbitrary interior point of the side $AB$. Let $M$ and $N$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $D$ to $BC$ and $AC$ respectively, and let $H_1$ and $H_2$ be the orthocenters of triangles $MNC$ and $MND$. Prove that the area of quadrilateral $AH_1BH_2$ does not depend on the position of $D$ on $AB$.

*Largest convenient set of squares that fails as soon as one square is removed*

> A set of unit squares of an $n \times n$ board is called convenient if every row and every column contains at least two squares of the set. For each $n \ge 5$, find the largest $m$ for which there is a convenient set of $m$ squares that ceases to be convenient when any one of its squares is removed.

**Metodo:** [[method_estremalita|Principio di estremalita]], [[method_casework|Case analysis]]

**Fonte:** apri PDF


<div class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></div>


*Massimo insieme comodo di caselle che cessa di esserlo togliendo una casella*

> Un insieme di caselle unitarie di una tabella $n \times n$ si dice comodo se ogni riga e ogni colonna contiene almeno due caselle dell'insieme. Per ogni $n \ge 5$ determinare il massimo $m$ per cui esiste un insieme comodo di $m$ caselle che cessa di essere comodo se si toglie una qualunque delle sue caselle.

*If P(Q(x))+P(R(x)) is constant then P or Q+R is constant*

> Let $P$, $Q$, $R$ be polynomials with real coefficients such that $P(Q(x))+P(R(x))$ is constant. Prove that $P(x)$ is constant or $Q(x)+R(x)$ is constant.
