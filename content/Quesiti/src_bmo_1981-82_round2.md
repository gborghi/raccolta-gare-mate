*Angle bisectors meet circumcircle; prove sum inequality*

*Sequence of primes; prove 5 never appears*

> The sequence $p_1, p_2, \ldots$ is defined as follows:
> 
> $p_1 = 2$, and for $n \geq 2$, $p_n$ is the largest prime divisor of
> $$p_1 p_2 \cdots p_{n-1} + 1.$$
> 
> Prove that $5$ is not a member of this sequence.

*Two points on curve x^n - ay^n = b; area of triangle bound*

> $P_1(x_1, y_1)$, $P_2(x_2, y_2)$ are two points on the curve $x^n - ay^n = b$ for which $x > 0$, $y > 0$. Here $a$ and $b$ are positive constants and $n$ is an integer $> 1$. Prove that if $y_1 < y_2$, and $\Delta$ is the area of triangle $OP_1P_2$, then
> $$by_2 > 2ny_1^{n-1} a^{1-\frac{1}{n}} \Delta.$$

*Polynomial satisfying functional relation; prove identity*

> Given that $k$ is a fixed non-negative integer and that the polynomial $P(x)$ satisfies the relation
> $$P(2x) = 2^{k-1}(P(x) + P(x+1)),$$
> prove that
> $$P(3x) = 3^{k-1}\left(P(x) + P\left(x+\tfrac{1}{3}\right) + P\left(x+\tfrac{2}{3}\right)\right).$$

**Topic:** [[topic_algebra|Algebra]], [[topic_insiemi_funzioni|Insiemi e funzioni]]
**Metodo:** [[method_induzione|Induzione]], [[method_simmetria|Simmetria]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_astrazione|Astrazione]], [[skill_riconoscimento_pattern|Riconoscimento di pattern]]
**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1e8i3xZYTs6OhsVopzbRKd7oUXgpnp9WB/view)


<span class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></span>


*Relazione funzionale polinomial soddisfacente; prova di identità*

> Dato che $k$ è un numero intero fisso non negativo e che il polinomio $P(x)$ soddisfa la relazione $$P(2x) = 2^{k-1}(P(x) + P(x+1)),$$ dimostra che $$P(3x) = 3^{k-1}\left(P(x) + P\left(x+\tfrac{1}{3}\right) + P\left(x+\tfrac{2}{3}\right)\right).$$
