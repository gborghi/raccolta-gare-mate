*A positive integer m is good if m equals n divided by the number of divisors of n, for some positive integer n. Prove 1,...,17 are good and 18 is not.*

> A positive integer $m$ is called good, if there is a positive integer $n$ such that $m$ is the quotient of $n$ over the number of positive integer divisors of $n$ (including 1 and $n$ itself). Prove that $1, 2, \cdots, 17$ are good numbers and that 18 is not a good number.

**Topic:** [[topic_aritmetica|Aritmetica / Teoria dei Numeri]]
**Metodo:** [[method_fattorizzazione|Fattorizzazione]], [[method_casework|Casework]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_conteggio_sistematico|Conteggio sistematico]]
**Area:** [[Aritmetica e Teoria dei Numeri]]
**Risposta:** 18 is not a good number
**Fonte:** [apri libro PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1A6F-Sk42BaQt80ts_sFXZiDY4uHJtL3I/view)

> [!warning] Estratto da verificare con la fonte.

*With D, E, F on the sides of acute triangle ABC and each of AD, BE, CF passing through the circumcenter, if any two of six side-segment ratios are integers, then ABC is isosceles.*

> Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle. Points $D$, $E$ and $F$ lie on segments $BC$, $CA$ and $AB$ respectively, and each of the three segments $AD$, $BE$ and $CF$ contains the circumcenter of $\triangle ABC$. Prove that if any two of the ratios $\frac{BD}{DC}, \frac{CE}{EA}, \frac{AF}{FB}, \frac{BF}{FA}, \frac{CD}{DB}, \frac{AE}{EC}$ are integers, then triangle $ABC$ is isosceles.

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]], [[topic_trigonometria|Trigonometria]]
**Metodo:** [[method_trigonometria|Trigonometria]], [[method_casework|Casework]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_ragionamento_geometrico|Ragionamento geometrico]], [[skill_casework_accurato|Casework accurato]]
**Area:** [[Geometria]]
**Risposta:** ABC is isosceles
**Fonte:** [apri libro PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1A6F-Sk42BaQt80ts_sFXZiDY4uHJtL3I/view)

> [!warning] Estratto da verificare con la fonte.

I punti $D$, $E$ e $F$ giacciono rispettivamente sui segmenti $BC$, $CA$ e $AB$, e ciascuno dei tre segmenti $AD$, $BE$ e $CF$ contiene il circocentro di $\triangle ABC$. Dimostrare che, se due qualsiasi dei rapporti $\frac{BD}{DC}, \frac{CE}{EA}, \frac{AF}{FB}, \frac{BF}{FA}, \frac{CD}{DB}, \frac{AE}{EC}$ sono numeri interi, allora il triangolo $ABC$ è isoscele.

*Minimize cyclic sum over nonneg reals summing to 2*

> Let $n$ be an integer greater than 3, and let $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ be nonnegative real numbers with $a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_n = 2$. Determine the minimum value of
> $$\frac{a_1}{a_2^2+1} + \frac{a_2}{a_3^2+1} + \cdots + \frac{a_n}{a_1^2+1}.$$

**Topic:** [[topic_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]], [[topic_algebra|Algebra]]
**Metodo:** [[method_induzione|Induzione]], [[method_disuguaglianze|Disuguaglianze]], [[method_simmetria|Simmetria]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_stima|Stima]], [[skill_ragionamento_geometrico|Ragionamento geometrico]]
**Area:** [[Algebra e Analisi]]
**Risposta:** $\frac{3}{2}$
**Fonte:** [apri libro PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1A6F-Sk42BaQt80ts_sFXZiDY4uHJtL3I/view)

*Prove DE perpendicular to EF in triangle with given angles*

> Point $D$ lies inside triangle $ABC$ such that $\angle DAC = \angle DCA = 30^\circ$ and $\angle DBA = 60^\circ$. Point $E$ is the midpoint of segment $BC$. Point $F$ lies on segment $AC$ with $AF = 2FC$. Prove that $DE \perp EF$.
