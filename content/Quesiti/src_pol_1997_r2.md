> For any real number $a$ find the number of ordered triples $(x, y, z)$ of real numbers which satisfy

*Minimum colors to color segments between n points, no two endpoints same color*

> Let be given $n$ points, no three of which are on a line. All the segments with endpoints in these points are colored so that two segments with a common endpoint are of different colors. Determine the least number of colors for which this is possible.

*Minimum di colori per i segmenti di colori tra n punti, non due punti finali dello stesso colore*
