*Collinearity of interior point and midpoints given BP=2PM*

> Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let $P$ be an interior point such that $\angle BPC = 90^\circ$, $\angle BAP = \angle BCP$. Let $M$, $N$ be the mid-points of $AC$, $BC$ respectively. Suppose $BP = 2PM$. Prove that $A$, $P$, $N$ are collinear.

*Sequence of 0/1 by parity of divisor count; rational or irrational?*

> Define a sequence $(a_n)_{n \ge 1}$ as follows:
> $$a_n = \begin{cases} 0, & \text{if the number of positive divisors of } n \text{ is odd,} \\ 1, & \text{if the number of positive divisors of } n \text{ is even.} \end{cases}$$
> (The positive divisors of $n$ include $1$ as well as $n$.) Let $x = 0.a_1 a_2 a_3 \ldots$ be the real number whose decimal expansion contains $a_n$ in the $n$-th place, $n \ge 1$. Determine, with proof, whether $x$ is rational or irrational.

*Sequenza di 0/1 in base alla parità del numero di divisori; razionale o irrazionale?*

> Definire una successione $(a_n)_{n \ge 1}$ nel modo seguente:
> $$a_n = \begin{cases} 0, & \text{if the number of positive divisors of } n \text{ is odd,} \\ 1, & \text{if the number of positive divisors of } n \text{ is even.} \end{cases}$$
> (I divisori positivi di $n$ includono $1$ nonché $n$.) Sia $x = 0.a_1 a_2 a_3 \ldots$ il numero reale la cui espansione decimale contiene $a_n$ alla $n$-esima cifra, $n \ge 1$. Determinare, con dimostrazione, se $x$ è razionale o irrazionale.
