*Chords AP AQ AR AS with equal angles; prove product identity*

> $AP$, $AQ$, $AR$, $AS$ are chords of a given circle with the property that $\angle PAQ = \angle QAR = \angle RAS$.
> 
> Prove that
> $$AR(AP + AR) = AQ(AQ + AS).$$

**Topic:** [[topic_geometria_piana|Geometria piana]]
**Metodo:** [[method_trigonometria|Trigonometria]], [[method_simmetria|Simmetria]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_ragionamento_geometrico|Ragionamento geometrico]], [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_lettura_attenta|Lettura attenta]]
**Area:** [[Geometria]]
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BVQrmCslxiyxp1Rg64CsxnMuP4Y7oEdJ/view)
