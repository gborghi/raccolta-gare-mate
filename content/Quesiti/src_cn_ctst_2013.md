*Find minimum of product involving gcd over n coprime integers*

> Given any $n\ (>1)$ coprime positive integers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$, denote $A = a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_n$. Let $d_i = (A,\, a_i)$ (the greatest common divisor), $i = 1, 2, \ldots, n$. Let $D_i$ be the greatest common divisor of $\{a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n\} \setminus \{a_i\}$, $i = 1, 2, \ldots, n$. Find the minimum of $\prod_{i=1}^{n} \dfrac{A - a_i}{d_i D_i}$.
> 
> (posed by Zhang Sihui)

*Trovare il minimo del prodotto che coinvolge il mcd su n interi coprimi tra loro*

> Dati due qualsiasi numeri interi positivi $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$ coprimi, $n\ (>1)$, si denoti $A = a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_n$. Sia $d_i = (A,\, a_i)$ (il massimo comune divisore), $i = 1, 2, \ldots, n$. Sia $D_i$ il massimo comune divisore di $\{a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n\} \setminus \{a_i\}$ e $i = 1, 2, \ldots, n$. Si determini il minimo di $\prod_{i=1}^{n} \dfrac{A - a_i}{d_i D_i}$.
>
> (proposto da Zhang Sihui)

*Min transitions so 101 persons each get 51 cards*

> Suppose there are 101 persons seated around a round table. Each person possesses $k$ pieces of cards, $k = 1, 2, \ldots, 101$. We call it a transition if one person transits one of his cards to one of his adjacent persons. Find the minimum positive number $r$ such that there is no way of more than $r$ transitions so that each person possesses 51 cards. (posed by Qu Zhenhua)

Definiamo transizione il caso in cui una persona cede una delle proprie carte a una delle persone adiacenti. *Existence of n with binomial coefficient congruence mod prime power*

> Let $p$ be a prime, $a$ and $b$ be positive integers satisfying $p^{2^a - 1} < 2p^b$. Prove that for any non-negative integer $k$, there exists a positive integer $n < p^{a+b}$ such that $n \equiv k \pmod{p^a}$ and $\binom{n}{k} \equiv k \pmod{p^b}$. (posed by Yu Hongbing)

*Prove inequality involving means and products of nonneg integers*

> Let $n \ge 2$ and $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n, b_1, b_2, \ldots, b_n$ be non-negative integers. Prove that
> $$\left(\frac{n}{n-1}\right)^{n-1}\left(\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i^2\right) + \left(\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} b_i\right)^2 \ge \prod_{i=1}^{n}(a_i^2 + b_i^2)^{\frac{1}{n}}.$$
> (posed by Leng Gangsong)
