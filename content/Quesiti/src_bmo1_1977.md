*Multiplicative f with digit-3 and f(10)=0 conditions implies f=0*

> A non-negative integer $f(n)$ is assigned to each positive integer $n$ in such a way that the following conditions are satisfied:
> (i) $f(mn) = f(m) + f(n)$ for all positive integers $m$, $n$;
> (ii) $f(n) = 0$ whenever the final (right-hand) decimal digit of $n$ is $3$; and
> (iii) $f(10) = 0$.
> Prove that $f(n) = 0$ for all positive integers $n$.

*Multiplicazione f con le condizioni di cifra 3 e f(10)=0 implica f=0*

> Un intero non negativo $f(n)$ è assegnato a ogni intero positivo $n$ in modo tale che siano soddisfatte le seguenti condizioni: (i) $f(mn) = f(m) + f(n)$ per tutti gli integri positivi $m$, $n$; (ii) $f(n) = 0$ quando la cifra decimale finale (a destra) di $n$ è $3$; e (iii) $f(10) = 0$. Prova che $f(n) = 0$ per tutti gli integri positivi $n$.

*Symmetric inequality in three non-negative reals; consequence for all reals*

> (i) Prove that, if $x$, $y$, $z$ are non-negative real numbers, then
> $$x(x-y)(x-z) + y(y-z)(y-x) + z(z-x)(z-y) \ge 0.$$
> (ii) Hence or otherwise show that for all real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$
> $$a^4 + b^4 + c^4 + 3a^2b^2c^2 \ge 2(b^2c^2 + c^2a^2 + a^2b^2).$$

*Two spheres each externally tangent to five vertex-spheres of octahedron; common point?*

> $A_1 A_2 A_3 A_4 A_5 A_6$ is a regular octahedron whose sides are each of length $2a$. For each $i = 1, 2, \ldots, 5$, $K_i$ is a sphere with centre $A_i$ and radius $r_i$. Each of these spheres $K_1, K_2, \ldots, K_5$ are all touched externally by each of two spheres $P_1$ and $P_2$ of radius $a$. Determine with proof and without tables whether $P_1$ and $P_2$ have or have not a common point.

*Decompose 26(x^{2n}+...+1) into polynomials with distinct digit coefficients; find n*

> The polynomial $26(x^{2n} + x^{2n-2} + \cdots + x^2 + 1)$, where $n > 1$, is to be decomposed into a sum of polynomials, each different. Each of these polynomials is of the form $a_k x^k + a_{k-1} x^{k-1} + \cdots + a_1 x + a_0$ where each $a_j$ is one of the numbers $1, 2, \ldots, 9$ and no two $a_j$ are equal.
> 
> Find all the values of $n$ for which this decomposition is possible.

*Decomponere 26(x^{2n}+...+1) in polinomi con diversi coefficienti digitali; trovare n*

Ciascuno di questi polinomi è di forma $a_k x^k + a_{k-1} x^{k-1} + \cdots + a_1 x + a_0$, dove ogni $a_j$ è uno dei numeri $1, 2, \ldots, 9$ e non ci sono due $a_j$ uguali.
> 
> Trova tutti i valori di $n$ per i quali è possibile questa decomposizione.
