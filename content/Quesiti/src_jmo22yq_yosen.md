> 
> Find the number of such colourings. (Colourings related by rotation or reflection are counted as different.)

*Cube side 2012 cut by plane as hexagon IJKLMN; find area combination*

> In space, a cube $ABCD\text{-}EFGH$ has side length $2012$. A plane intersects the cube and the cross-section is a hexagon $IJKLMN$, where $I$, $J$, $K$, $L$, $M$, $N$ lie on edges $AE$, $EF$, $FG$, $GC$, $CD$, $DA$ respectively.
> 
> Given $AI - GL = 8$, $CM - EJ = 6$, $FK - DN = 4$, find
> $$(\text{area of } \triangle IKM) + (\text{area of } \triangle JLN) - (\text{area of hexagon } IJKLMN).$$
> 
> (Here, $XY$ denotes the length of segment $XY$.)
