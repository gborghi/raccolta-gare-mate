*Find three positive integers satisfying two linear conditions*

> *N.B. In this exercise, and only here, only an answer is required without justification.*
> 
> Let $m > n > p$ be three positive integers such that $m + n + p = 74$ and $m - n - p = 44$. Determine $m$, $n$, and $p$.
> 
> (A prime number is a strictly positive integer greater than $1$ whose only divisors are $1$ and itself.)

**Topic:** [[topic_aritmetica|Aritmetica / Teoria dei Numeri]], [[topic_algebra|Algebra]]
**Metodo:** [[method_casework|Casework]]
**Abilita:** [[skill_manipolazione_algebrica|Manipolazione algebrica]], [[skill_lettura_attenta|Lettura attenta]]
**Area:** [[Aritmetica e Teoria dei Numeri]], [[Algebra e Analisi]]
**Risposta:** m=59, n=10, p=5
**Fonte:** [apri PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1WRDBVKTu2eO-0ruMO9lo0GjGJQSpzivB/view)

*Perpendicular bisectors in 60-degree triangle give equal segment*

> Let $ABC$ be a triangle such that $\widehat{BAC} = 60^\circ$. The perpendicular bisector of $[AC]$ meets line $(AB)$ at $P$, and the perpendicular bisector of $[AB]$ meets line $(AC)$ at $Q$. Show that $PQ = BC$.

*I bisettori perpendicolari in un triangolo di 60 gradi danno segmento uguale*

*Friendship alignment: at most 2 friends yields spacing ≤2017; 3 friends may fail*

> A mathematics camp has exactly one million students; some pairs of students are friends (if $A$ is a friend of $B$, then $B$ is a friend of $A$).
> 
> a) Suppose that each student has at most two friends. Show that it is possible to line up all the students in a row such that between any two friends there are at most $2017$ other students between them.
> 
> b) Suppose now that each student has at most three friends. Show that this is not necessarily possible.
