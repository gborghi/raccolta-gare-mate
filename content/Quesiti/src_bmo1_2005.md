*Find positive integer n for Paul-Jenny money exchange*

> Each of Paul and Jenny has a whole number of pounds. She says to him: "If you give me $\pounds 3$, I will have $n$ times as much as you." He says to her: "If you give me $\pounds n$, I will have $3$ times as much as you." Given that all these statements are true and that $n$ is a positive integer, what are the possible values for $n$?

*Prove CP = CQ using altitude-foot semicircles on triangle sides*

> Let $ABC$ be an acute-angled triangle, and let $D$, $E$ be the feet of the altitudes from $A$, $B$ to $BC$, $CA$ respectively. Let $P$ be the point where the line $AD$ meets the semicircle constructed outwardly on $BC$, and $Q$ be the point where the line $BE$ meets the semicircle constructed outwardly on $AC$. Prove that $CP = CQ$.

*Prove CP = CQ utilizzando semicircoli di altitudine a piedi sui lati del triangolo*

Prove che $CP = CQ$.

*Least n so any 2-colouring of {1,...,n} yields monochromatic x+y+z=w*

> Determine the least natural number $n$ for which the following result holds: No matter how the elements of the set $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$ are coloured red or blue, there are integers $x, y, z$ in the set (not necessarily distinct) of the same colour such that $x + y + z = w$.

<span class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></span>


*Almeno n in modo che qualsiasi 2 colori di {1,...,n} producano una monocromatica x+y+z=w*

> Determinare il numero naturale minimo $n$ per il quale è valido il seguente risultato: Indipendentemente dal colore rosso o blu degli elementi del set $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$, nel set (non necessariamente distinto) ci sono enti $x, y, z$ dello stesso colore, come $x + y + z = w$.

*Set of rationals closed under two maps contains all rationals in (0,1)*

> Let $S$ be a set of rational numbers with the following properties:
> (i) $\frac{1}{2} \in S$,
> (ii) If $x \in S$, then both $\frac{1}{x+1} \in S$ and $\frac{x}{x+1} \in S$.
> Prove that $S$ contains all rational numbers in the interval $0 < x < 1$.

<span class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></span>


*Un insieme di razionali chiusi sotto due mappe contiene tutti i razionali in (0,1) *

> Se $S$ è un insieme di numeri razionali con le seguenti proprietà: (i) $\frac{1}{2} \in S$, (ii) Se $x \in S$, allora sia $\frac{1}{x+1} \in S$ che $\frac{x}{x+1} \in S$. Prova che $S$ contiene tutti i numeri razionali nell'intervallo $0 < x < 1$.
