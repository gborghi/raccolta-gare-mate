*Find limiting value of ratio of sums involving binomial coefficients*

> Find, with proof, the value of the limit as $n \to \infty$ of
> $$\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n} \binom{2n}{r}^{-1} \cdot 2^r}{\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n} \binom{2n}{r+1}^{-1} \cdot 2^r}.$$
> Here $\binom{2n}{r}$ denotes a binomial coefficient.

**Metodo:** [[method_telescoping|Telescoping]], [[method_induzione|Induzione]]

<div class="qlang-split" data-lang="it"></div>


*Trovare il valore limite del rapporto di somme che coinvolgono coefficienti binomiali*

> Dimostrare che, se e solo se, il valore del limite per $n \to \infty$ di
> $$\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n} \binom{2n}{r}^{-1} \cdot 2^r}{\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n} \binom{2n}{r+1}^{-1} \cdot 2^r}.$$
> Qui $\binom{2n}{r}$ denota un coefficiente binomiale.

*Polynomial with integer coefficients; prove N=26 given P(N)=N+51*

> Let $P(x)$ be any polynomial with integer coefficients such that
> $$P(21) = 17, \quad P(32) = -247, \quad P(37) = 33.$$
> Prove that if $P(N) = N + 51$ for some integer $N$, then $N = 26$.
